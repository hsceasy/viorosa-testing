Lesson 01

Wages and penalty rates

Multiply hours by the applicable hourly rate, then add earnings.

Worked example

Work 30 hours at $24 and 4 overtime hours at time-and-a-half.

Overtime rate = 24 × 1.5 = $36
Ordinary pay = 30 × 24 = $720
Overtime pay = 4 × 36 = $144
Total = 720 + 144 = $864

Now you try

Work 20 hours at $25 and 4 hours at double time.

Show worked solution

Double rate = 25 × 2 = $50
Total = 20 × 25 + 4 × 50
= 500 + 200
= $700

Lesson 02

Commission, piecework and royalties

Commission and royalties use a percentage; piecework uses a rate per item.

Worked example

Earn 4% commission on $3500 sales and $2 per 80 items.

Commission = 0.04 × 3500 = $140
Piecework = 2 × 80 = $160
Total = 140 + 160 = $300

Now you try

Earn 5% royalties on $6000 sales.

Show worked solution

Royalty = 5100 × 6000
= 0.05 × 6000
= $300

Lesson 03

Pay periods

Use 52 weeks, 26 fortnights and 12 months per year.

Worked example

Convert $850 weekly to monthly.

Annual = 850 × 52 = $44,200
Monthly = 4420012
≈ $3683.33

Now you try

Convert $1200 fortnightly to annual.

Show worked solution

Annual = 1200 × 26
= $31,200

Lesson 04

Leave loading

Apply the specified percentage to eligible ordinary leave pay.

Worked example

Find 17.5% loading on $1600 leave pay.

Loading = 0.175 × 1600
= $280
Total = 1600 + 280
= $1880

Now you try

Find loading on $2000 at 17.5%.

Show worked solution

Loading = 17.5100 × 2000
= 0.175 × 2000
= $350

PAYG withholding

Use the ATO tax withheld calculator or current tables for the relevant pay period and circumstances. Example withholding is supplied, not a current tax quote.

Worked example

Weekly gross $900; supplied PAYG withholding $120. Find pay after withholding.

Pay after withholding = 900 − 120
= $780

Show worked solution

1100 − 180
= $920

Lesson 06

Taxable income and tax

Subtract allowable deductions from assessable income. Apply current ATO marginal rates in a real investigation; this example uses a hypothetical schedule.

Worked example

Income $50,000, allowable deductions $2000. Hypothetical tax: 0% on first $20,000 and 20% above.

Taxable income = 50000 − 2000 = $48000
Amount in second bracket = 48000 − 20000 = $28000
Tax = 0.20 × 28000 = $5600

Australian Taxation Office — current tax tables and calculators

Now you try

Income $40,000 and allowable deductions $1000. Find taxable income.

Show worked solution

40000 − 1000
= $39000

Lesson 07

Net earnings

Subtract tax and other payroll deductions from gross pay.

Worked example

Gross $1200, PAYG $180, other deductions $40.

Net = 1200 − 180 − 40
= 1020 − 40
= $980

Now you try

Gross $800, PAYG $90, deductions $20.

Show worked solution

Net = 800 − 90 − 20
= 710 − 20
= $690

Lesson 08

Simple interest

I = Prn, where r is a decimal rate per period and n is the number of matching periods.

Worked example

Invest $2000 at 4% p.a. for 3 years.

I = Prn
= 2000 × 0.04 × 3
= 80 × 3
= $240
Balance = 2000 + 240 = $2240

Now you try

Invest $1500 at 6% p.a. for 2 years. Find interest.

Show worked solution

I = 1500 × 0.06 × 2
= 90 × 2
= $180

Lesson 09

Simple interest graphs and unknowns

Simple interest increases by a constant amount per period. The gradient of the balance graph is Pr.

Worked example

A $1000 investment earns $50 per year. Find the rate.

50 = 1000r
501000 = 1000r1000
r = 0.05
= 5%

Now you try

A $2000 investment earns $80 yearly. Find rate.

Show worked solution

80 = 2000r
r = 802000
= 0.04
= 4%

Lesson 10

Buying on terms

Total cost is deposit plus all instalments and fees.

Worked example

Deposit $200, 12 monthly payments of $85. Cash price $1100.

Total = 200 + 12 × 85
= 200 + 1020
= $1220
Extra cost = 1220 − 1100 = $120

Now you try

Deposit $100 and 10 payments of $60. Find total.

Show worked solution

Total = 100 + 10 × 60
= 100 + 600
= $700

Lesson 11

Buy now pay later

Compare total repayments including any applicable fees; fees here are illustrative.

Worked example

A $240 purchase has four $60 instalments and one $10 late fee.

Total = 4 × 60 + 10
= 240 + 10
= $250

Now you try

Four $45 instalments and $8 fee. Find total.

Show worked solution

4 × 45 + 8
= 180 + 8
= $188

Lesson 12

Short-term loans

Compare borrowing costs over the same loan amount and duration, including fees.

Worked example

Borrow $400. Option A repays $460; B repays $440 plus $30 fee.

A cost = 460 − 400 = $60
B total = 440 + 30 = $470
B cost = 470 − 400 = $70
A costs $10 less.

Now you try

Borrow $300 and repay $345 including fees. Find cost.

Show worked solution

Cost = 345 − 300
= $45
