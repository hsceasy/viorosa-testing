Lesson 03

Instrument precision

The smallest marked division determines the instrument resolution.

Worked example

A ruler has 10 equal intervals between 2 and 3 cm. Find resolution.

Resolution = 3 − 210
= 0.1 cm
= 1 mm

Now you try

A scale has 5 equal intervals from 0 to 10 g. Find resolution.

Show worked solution

105
= 2 g

Lesson 04

Absolute error

For a reading rounded to the nearest division, maximum absolute error is half that division.

Worked example

A length is recorded to the nearest millimetre. Find maximum error.

Error = 12
= 0.5 mm

Now you try

Find maximum error for nearest 2 g.

Show worked solution

Error = 22
= 1 g

Lesson 05

Percentage error

Divide maximum absolute error by measured value and multiply by 100%.

Worked example

A reading is 20 cm with maximum error 0.5 cm. Find percentage error.

0.520 × 100%
= 0.025 × 100%
= 2.5%

Now you try

A reading is 50 g with error 1 g. Find percentage error.

Show worked solution

150 × 100%
= 0.02 × 100%
= 2%

Lesson 06

Significant figures

Count from the first nonzero digit; round using the next digit.

Worked example

Round 0.004786 to 3 significant figures.

First three significant digits: 4, 7, 8
Next digit = 6, round up
≈ 0.00479

Now you try

Round 5836 to 2 significant figures.

Show worked solution

First two digits: 5, 8
Next digit = 3, keep 8
≈ 5800

Lesson 07

Rounding and truncation

Truncation drops digits; rounding uses the next digit. Keep extra digits during calculations.

Worked example

Compare 3.678 rounded and truncated to 2 decimal places.

Rounded: next digit 8 → 3.68
Truncated: 3.67

Now you try

Round 2.999 to 2 decimal places.

Show worked solution

Next digit = 9
≈ 3.00

Lesson 11

Estimating calculations

Round coefficients to convenient values and apply index laws.

Worked example

Estimate (3.9 × 10⁵)(2.1 × 10³).

≈ (4 × 10⁵)(2 × 10³)
= 8 × 10^(5 + 3)
= 8 × 10⁸

Now you try

Estimate (6.2 × 10⁴) ÷ (2.1 × 10²).

Show worked solution

≈ 62 × 10^(4 − 2)
= 3 × 10²

Lesson 12

Scientific notation calculations

Use index laws or a calculator EXP key; normalise the result and round only at the end.

Worked example

Calculate (6 × 10⁴)(4 × 10⁵).

= (6 × 4) × 10^(4 + 5)
= 24 × 10⁹
= 2.4 × 10¹⁰

Now you try

Calculate (8 × 10⁶) ÷ (4 × 10²).

Show worked solution

= 84 × 10^(6 − 2)
= 2 × 10⁴
