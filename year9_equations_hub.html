Lesson 01

One-step equations

Keep both sides balanced by applying the same inverse operation.

Worked example

Solve x + 7 = 12.

x + 7 − 7 = 12 − 7
x = 5

Now you try

Solve x − 4 = 9.

Show worked solution

x − 4 + 4 = 9 + 4
x = 13

Lesson 02

Negative coefficients

Divide both sides by the signed coefficient.

Worked example

Solve −3x = 18.

−3x−3 = 18−3
x = −6

Now you try

Solve −4x = 28.

Show worked solution

−4x−4 = 28−4
x = −7

Lesson 03

Two-step equations

Undo addition or subtraction before division.

Worked example

Solve 3x + 5 = 20.

3x + 5 − 5 = 20 − 5
3x = 15
3x3 = 153
x = 5

Now you try

Solve 4x − 3 = 17.

Show worked solution

4x − 3 + 3 = 17 + 3
4x = 20
4x4 = 204
x = 5

Lesson 04

Grouping symbols

Expand or divide out a common factor first.

Worked example

Solve 3(x + 2) = 21.

3(x + 2)3 = 213
x + 2 = 7
x + 2 − 2 = 7 − 2
x = 5

Now you try

Solve 2(x − 4) = 14.

Show worked solution

2(x − 4)2 = 142
x − 4 = 7
x − 4 + 4 = 7 + 4
x = 11

Lesson 05

Unknowns on both sides

Collect variable terms on one side.

Worked example

Solve 5x + 2 = 3x + 12.

5x + 2 − 3x = 3x + 12 − 3x
2x + 2 = 12
2x + 2 − 2 = 12 − 2
2x = 10
2x2 = 102
x = 5

Now you try

Solve 6x − 4 = 2x + 16.

Show worked solution

6x − 4 − 2x = 2x + 16 − 2x
4x − 4 = 16
4x − 4 + 4 = 16 + 4
4x = 20
4x4 = 204
x = 5

Now you try

Show worked solution

Lesson 07

Fraction with grouped numerator

Clear the denominator before isolating the variable.

Worked example

Solve 2x + 35 = 7.

5 × 2x + 35 = 5 × 7
2x + 3 = 35
2x + 3 − 3 = 35 − 3
2x = 32
2x2 = 322
x = 16

Now you try

Solve 3x − 24 = 7.

Show worked solution

4 × 3x − 24 = 4 × 7
3x − 2 = 28
3x − 2 + 2 = 28 + 2
3x = 30
3x3 = 303
x = 10

Lesson 08

x3 + 4 − 4 = 9 − 4
x3 = 5

Now you try

Solve x5 − 2 = 4.

Show worked solution

x5 − 2 + 2 = 4 + 2

Lesson 09

Verifying solutions

Substitute into both original sides.

Worked example

Check x = 4 in 3x + 2 = 14.

LHS = 3(4) + 2
= 12 + 2
= 14
RHS = 14
LHS = RHS

Now you try

Check x = 3 in 5x − 2 = 13.

Show worked solution

LHS = 5(3) − 2
= 15 − 2
= 13
RHS = 13
LHS = RHS

Lesson 10

Substitution into formulas

Replace known quantities then solve for the unknown.

Worked example

A = bh, A = 48 and b = 6. Find h.

48 = 6h
486 = 6h6
8 = h

Now you try

P = 2l + 2w, P = 30 and l = 9. Find w.

Show worked solution

30 = 2(9) + 2w
30 = 18 + 2w
30 − 18 = 18 + 2w − 18
12 = 2w
122 = 2w2
6 = w

Lesson 11

Word problems

Define the unknown and interpret the solution.

Worked example

A taxi charges $5 plus $3 per km. Total $26. Find distance.

Let x be kilometres.
5 + 3x = 26
5 + 3x − 5 = 26 − 5
3x = 21
3x3 = 213
x = 7 km

Now you try

A ticket costs $4 plus $2 per ride. Total $18. Find rides.

Show worked solution

Let x be rides.
4 + 2x = 18
4 + 2x − 4 = 18 − 4
2x = 14
2x2 = 142
x = 7 rides

Lesson 12

Mixed applications

Choose efficient inverse operations and check in the original equation.

Worked example

Solve 4(x − 1) = 2x + 10.

4x − 4 = 2x + 10
4x − 4 − 2x = 2x + 10 − 2x
2x − 4 = 10
2x − 4 + 4 = 10 + 4
2x = 14
2x2 = 142
x = 7
Check: 4(7 − 1) = 24 and 2(7) + 10 = 24.

Now you try

Solve 3(x + 2) = x + 14.

Show worked solution

3x + 6 = x + 14
3x + 6 − x = x + 14 − x
2x + 6 = 14
2x + 6 − 6 = 14 − 6
2x = 8
2x2 = 82
x = 4
Check: 3(4 + 2) = 18 and 4 + 14 = 18.
