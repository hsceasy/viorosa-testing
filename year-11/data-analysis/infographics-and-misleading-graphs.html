Infographics and misleading graphs

Learn the concept

Infographics combine visual displays and explanatory text. Check source, dates, denominator, units, scale and uncertainty. Truncated axes, unequal intervals, pictogram area and selective dates can mislead.

Worked example

In an illustrative school survey, 40% walked last year and 44% this year: an increase of 4 percentage points, or 10% relative. A truncated axis can exaggerate the change. A column graph with zero baseline communicates the comparison fairly.

Now you try

Does this establish that every student walks more?

Show worked answer

No; these are aggregate proportions, and sample composition may differ.
