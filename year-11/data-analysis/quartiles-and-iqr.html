Quartiles and interquartile range

Learn the concept

Five-number summary: minimum, Q1, median, Q3, maximum. Use the median of each half, excluding the overall median for odd N. IQR = Q3 − Q1, measuring the middle 50%; range uses extremes.

Worked example

Ordered data 2, 3, 4, 5, 5, 5, 6, 7, 8: Q1 = 3.5, median = 5, Q3 = 6.5, IQR = 3, range = 6. Quartiles can also be estimated from cumulative frequency graphs.

Now you try

Find the five-number summary for 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12.

Show worked answer

2, 4, 7, 10, 12; IQR = 6.
