Grouped and ungrouped data

Learn the concept

Ungrouped data retains individual values. Grouping many measurements into non-overlapping intervals reveals patterns but loses exact values. Use equal-width intervals for ordinary frequency histograms.

Worked example

Times 12, 15, 18, 21, 24, 24, 29, 32, 35, 39 give frequencies 3, 4, 3 in intervals 10–<20, 20–<30, 30–<40. The modal class is 20–<30; the exact mean cannot be recovered from grouped counts alone.

Now you try

Estimate the mean using class midpoints.

Show worked answer

(15×3 + 25×4 + 35×3) ÷ 10 = 25 minutes.
