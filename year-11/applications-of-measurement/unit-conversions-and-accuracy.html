Unit conversions and accuracy

Key idea

Multiply by the length factor for each dimension. From metres to centimetres the factor is 100; reverse by dividing.

Length

1 m = 100 cm

Area

1 m² = 100 × 100 = 10 000 cm²

Volume

1 m³ = 100 × 100 × 100 = 1 000 000 cm³

Worked example 1

Convert 2.5 m² to cm².

A = 2.5 × 100²

A = 2.5 × 10 000

A = 25 000 cm²

Worked example 2

Convert 0.04 m³ to cm³.

V = 0.04 × 100³

V = 0.04 × 1 000 000

V = 40 000 cm³

Accuracy

A length is rounded to 12 cm to the nearest centimetre. Find the bounds.

Lower bound = 12 − 0.5 = 11.5 cm

Upper bound = 12 + 0.5 = 12.5 cm

11.5 cm ≤ length < 12.5 cm.
