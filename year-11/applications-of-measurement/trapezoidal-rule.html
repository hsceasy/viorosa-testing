Trapezoidal rule

Key idea

To estimate the area of an irregular shape, split it into strips with equal spacing. Pretend the curved edges are straight between measurements. Add the areas of the trapeziums.

Equal spacing h

A ≈ h2 × [first width + last width + 2 × (sum of middle widths)]

Dam surface

Widths across a dam at 4 m intervals are 3, 5, 6 and 4 m. Estimate its area.

A ≈ 42 × [3 + 4 + 2(5 + 6)]

A ≈ 2 × [7 + 22]

A ≈ 2 × 29

A ≈ 58 m²
