Perimeter, area and composite shapes

Key idea

Perimeter is the distance around the outside. Area measures the space inside. Heights are perpendicular to bases; kite diagonals cross at right angles.

Rectangle

A = lw

Triangle

A = bh2

Parallelogram

A = bh

Trapezium

A = h(a+b)2

Kite

A = d₁d₂2

Composite patio

A 10 m by 7 m patio has a 3 m by 2 m corner removed. Find area and perimeter.

A = 10 × 7 − 3 × 2

A = 70 − 6

A = 64 m²

P = 10 + 5 + 3 + 2 + 7 + 7

P = 34 m
