Circle arcs and sectors

Key idea

r is radius, d is diameter, θ is the central angle in degrees.

Circumference

C = 2πr = πd

Circle area

A = πr²

Arc length

L = θ360 × 2πr

Sector area

A = θ360 × πr²

Sector perimeter

P = 2r + L

Garden sector

A garden has radius 6 m and central angle 90°. Find arc length, area and perimeter.

L = 90360 × 2π × 6

L = 3π ≈ 9.42 m

A = 90360 × π × 6²

A = 9π ≈ 28.27 m²

P = 2 × 6 + 3π

P ≈ 21.42 m
