Longitude and time differences

Key idea

Earth rotates approximately 360° in 24 hours. Locations east are ahead in theoretical solar time; west are behind. Actual time zones may differ.

Rotation

360° ÷ 24 = 15° per hour

Time difference

Difference in longitude (°)15 hours

Use positive east and negative west longitudes. Subtract the signed longitudes and take the magnitude for the time difference.

Opposite hemispheres

A is at 30° W and B is at 75° E. At A it is 09:00. Find theoretical time at B.

Difference = 75 − (−30)

= 105°

Time difference = 105 ÷ 15

= 7 hours

B is east, so 09:00 + 7 h = 16:00.

Same hemisphere

A is at 120° E and B at 82.5° E. At A it is 14:00.

Difference = 120 − 82.5 = 37.5°

Time difference = 37.5 ÷ 15 = 2.5 h

0.5 × 60 = 30 min

B is west: 14:00 − 2 h 30 min = 11:30.
