VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 11 · MATHEMATICS STANDARD

Commission, piecework and royalties

Key idea

Commission

Sales × commission rate

Piecework

Number of items × payment per item

Royalties

Sales revenue × royalty rate

Commission

A salesperson earns $500 base pay plus 4% of $8,750 in sales. Find total pay.

Commission = 0.04 × 8,750

= $350

Total = 500 + 350

= $850

Piecework

A worker packs 145 boxes at $2.40 each. Find earnings.

Pay = 145 × 2.40

= $348

Royalties

An author receives 7% of the selling price of each book. 850 books sell for $24 each. Find royalties.

Sales revenue = 850 × 24

= $20,400

Royalties = 0.07 × 20,400

= $1,428
