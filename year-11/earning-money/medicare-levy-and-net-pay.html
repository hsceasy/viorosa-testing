Medicare levy and net pay

Key idea

Use the levy rate and conditions supplied in the question. Net pay is gross pay less employee deductions.

Worked example 1

Simplified levy of 2% on $50,000 → $1000.

Worked example 2

Gross $1200 less $180 PAYG and $20 deductions → $1000 net.

Common mistake: Subtracting employer super from net pay.
