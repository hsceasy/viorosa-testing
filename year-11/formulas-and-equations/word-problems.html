Word problems

Key idea

Define a variable, translate the information into an equation, solve it and interpret the answer in context. Check the result against the original situation.

Taxi fare

A taxi charges $5 plus $3 per kilometre. The fare is $29. Find the distance.

Let x be the distance in km.

5+3x = 29

3x = 24

x = 8

The journey was 8 km. Check: 5+3(8) = 29.

Compare two plans

Plan A costs $12 plus $4 per visit. Plan B costs $24 plus $2 per visit. When are they equal?

Let x be the number of visits.

12+4x = 24+2x

12+2x = 24

2x = 12

x = 6

Both plans cost $36 at six visits.

Check units, brackets and each inverse operation before finalising your answer.
