Shortest paths and their limitations

From A to C, the direct edge has weight 3; via B the weight is 2 + 2 = 4. The shortest path is AC. However, the unique minimum spanning tree uses AB and BC (total 4); AC is not in any minimum spanning tree of this network. A shortest distance may also be a poor choice if a road has tolls, congestion or closures.

Try it: shortest path in the four-building network

Find the shortest route from B to D.

Answer: B–C–D has weight 7, compared with B–A–D (8) and B–A–C–D (11).
