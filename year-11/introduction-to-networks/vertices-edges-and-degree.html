Vertices edges and degree

Key idea

Vertices are points and edges connect them. Degree counts incident edge ends; a loop counts twice.

Worked example 1

Triangle ABC has three vertices, three edges, degree 2 each.

Worked example 2

A vertex with two ordinary incident edges and a loop has degree 4.

Common mistake: Counting a loop only once.
