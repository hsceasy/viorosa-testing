Quadratic and cubic functions

Key ideas

A quadratic graph is a parabola. It opens up if a > 0 and down if a < 0.

The discriminant determines how many x-intercepts exist. A negative discriminant means the parabola never crosses the x-axis.

The axis of symmetry is x = −b/(2a). Substitute this x-value to find the vertex. Completing the square also reveals the vertex.

To find intersections, equate the two function rules and solve. To prove two quadratics identical, match corresponding coefficients.

To solve a quadratic inequality, find the roots and use the graph to identify the intervals above or below the x-axis.

A cubic y = a(x − h)³ + k has its central inflection point at (h, k).

Sketching a quadratic

  1. Find the direction of opening.
  2. Find the x-intercepts if they exist, and the y-intercept.
  3. Find the axis of symmetry and vertex.
  4. Plot these features and draw a smooth parabola.

Worked example — quadratic graph

Sketch y = x² − 4x + 3 and solve y < 0.

Solution

\[y=(x-2)^2-1\]

Vertex (2, −1); axis x = 2; y-intercept (0, 3).

\[0=(x-1)(x-3)\]

\[x=1\text{ or }3\]

\[1<x<3\]

The parabola is below the x-axis between its roots.

Worked example — intersections

Find where y = x² − 4x + 3 meets y = x − 1.

Solution

\[x^2-4x+3=x-1\]

\[x^2-5x+4=0\]

\[(x-1)(x-4)=0\]

\[x=1\text{ or }4\]

Substitute into y = x − 1. The points are (1, 0) and (4, 3).

Worked example — quadratic formula

Solve 2x² + x − 4 = 0.

Solution

\[x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}\]

\[x=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{1+32}}{4}\]

\[x=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{33}}4\]

Graph transformations

For y = a(x − h)² + k or y = a(x − h)³ + k, h shifts horizontally and k vertically. The magnitude of a scales the height; negative a reflects in the x-axis.

Adding a number outside the function moves the graph up; subtracting moves it down.

Replacing x with x − h moves the graph right h units; replacing x with x + h moves it left h units (h positive).

Multiplying the output by 2 doubles its height from the x-axis; multiplying by ½ halves it. A negative multiplier also reflects it in the x-axis.

Replacing x by 2x compresses horizontally by factor ½; replacing x by x/2 stretches horizontally by factor 2. Replacing x by −x reflects in the y-axis.
