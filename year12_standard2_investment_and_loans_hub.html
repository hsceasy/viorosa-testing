Formula guide

Simple interest

I = Prn; final balance = P + I.

Compound interest

FV = PV(1 + r)n. Use the interest rate and number of periods consistently.

Depreciation

Straight-line: S = V₀ − Dn. Declining-balance: S = V₀(1 − r)n.

Dividend yield

Dividend yield (%) = dividend per share ÷ share price × 100.

Reducing-balance loan

Closing balance = opening balance + interest − repayment. Unless specified otherwise, repayments are at the end of each period.

Rounding

Round money to cents. For monthly loan tables, round interest to cents each month as instructed. A final loan repayment can be smaller than the regular repayment.
