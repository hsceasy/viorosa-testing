Key terms
commission
Payment based on a percentage of sales.
deduction
Amount subtracted from income or pay; allowable tax deductions reduce taxable income.
government payments
Financial support paid by government; some payments are taxable.
gross pay
Total pay before deductions.
leave loading
Extra pay on eligible annual leave.
Medicare levy
Contribution towards Medicare, generally 2% of taxable income, subject to reductions and exemptions.
model
Simplified mathematical representation of a real situation.
net earnings
Gross earnings minus deductions.
overtime
Work beyond ordinary hours, often paid at a higher rate.
PAYG tax
Pay-As-You-Go withholding: tax withheld during the year.
penalty rate
Higher pay rate for specified hours or conditions.
piecework
Payment per item produced or task completed.
refund
Money returned when withholding exceeds final tax liability.
royalties
Payments for use or sale of intellectual property.
salary
Fixed annual pay, usually paid in instalments.
tax
Compulsory payment to government.
tax return
Annual declaration of income, deductions and tax credits.
taxable income
Assessable income minus allowable deductions.
wage
Pay based on hours worked or output.
budget
Plan for income and spending.
buy now, pay later
Purchase now and pay in instalments later.
deposit
Initial part-payment or money placed in an account.
discretionary spending
Optional spending.
fixed spending
Regular expenses that are generally predictable.
GST
Goods and services tax; generally 10% on taxable Australian sales.
loss
Cost price minus selling price when cost is higher.
percentage decrease
Reduction divided by original amount, multiplied by 100%.
percentage increase
Increase divided by original amount, multiplied by 100%.
profit
Selling price minus cost price when selling price is higher.
stamp duty
State or territory tax on certain transactions.
VAT
Value-added tax on goods and services.
