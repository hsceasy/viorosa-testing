Key terms
Perimeter
Distance around a shape.
Area
Surface enclosed by a shape.
Volume
Space occupied by a solid.
Capacity
Amount a container can hold.
Radius
Distance from centre to circle.
Diameter
Distance across a circle through its centre.
Circumference
Perimeter of a circle.
Prism
Solid with constant cross-section.
Perpendicular height
Height at right angles to the base.
Composite shape
Shape made from simpler shapes.

Metric length

Multiply when converting to smaller units; divide when converting to larger units.

Worked example

Convert 2.4 m to cm.

1 m = 100 cm
2.4 × 100
= 240 cm

Now you try

Convert 3500 m to km.

Show full solution
1 km = 1000 m
3500 ÷ 1000
= 3.5 km

Perimeter of polygons

Add the lengths around the outside.

Worked example

Rectangle: 9 cm by 4 cm.

P = 2 × (l + w)
= 2 × (9 + 4)
= 2 × 13
= 26 cm

Now you try

Rectangle: 7 m by 3 m.

Show full solution
P = 2 × (7 + 3)
= 2 × 10
= 20 m

Composite perimeter

Find missing sides before adding exterior edges.

Worked example

A 10 by 7 cm rectangle has a 3 by 2 cm corner removed.

Missing horizontal = 10 − 3 = 7 cm
Missing vertical = 7 − 2 = 5 cm
P = 7 + 2 + 3 + 5 + 10 + 7
= 34 cm

Now you try

A 9 by 6 cm rectangle has a 2 by 1 cm corner removed.

Show full solution
Missing horizontal = 9 − 2 = 7 cm
Missing vertical = 6 − 1 = 5 cm
P = 7 + 1 + 2 + 5 + 9 + 6
= 30 cm

Area units

Square the length conversion factor.

Worked example

Convert 3 m² to cm².

1 m² = 100 × 100 cm²
= 10 000 cm²
3 × 10 000
= 30 000 cm²

Now you try

Convert 45000 cm² to m².

Show full solution
45 000 ÷ 10 000
= 4.5 m²

Rectangles and triangles

Area measures the surface enclosed. Use perpendicular height.

Worked example

Triangle: base 8 cm, height 5 cm.

A = 12 × b × h
= 12 × 8 × 5
= 4 × 5
= 20 cm²

Now you try

Rectangle: 8 cm by 5 cm.

Show full solution
A = l × w
= 8 × 5
= 40 cm²

Parallelograms

Use the perpendicular height, not the sloping side.

Worked example

Base 9 cm, perpendicular height 4 cm.

A = b × h
= 9 × 4
= 36 cm²

Now you try

Base 12 m, height 3 m.

Show full solution
A = 12 × 3
= 36 m²

Trapeziums, rhombuses and kites

For a trapezium average the parallel sides then multiply by height. For a kite or rhombus use half the product of diagonals.

Trapezium

Worked example

Trapezium: parallel sides 6 and 10 cm, height 4 cm.

A = 12 × (a + b) × h
= 12 × (6 + 10) × 4
= 8 × 4
= 32 cm²

Now you try

A trapezium has parallel sides of 8 cm and 12 cm and a perpendicular height of 5 cm. Find its area.

Show full solution
A = 12 × (a + b) × h
= 12 × (8 + 12) × 5
= 12 × 20 × 5
= 10 × 5
= 50 cm²

Rhombus

A rhombus has four equal sides. Its diagonals meet at right angles and bisect each other. Multiply the full lengths of the diagonals and halve the result.

Worked example

Find the area of the rhombus shown.

A = 12 × d₁ × d₂
= 12 × 10 × 6
= 12 × 60
= 30 cm²

Now you try

Find the area of a rhombus with diagonals 12 cm and 7 cm.

Show full solution
A = 12 × d₁ × d₂
= 12 × 12 × 7
= 12 × 84
= 42 cm²

Kite

A kite has two pairs of equal adjacent sides. Its diagonals meet at right angles. Multiply the full lengths of the diagonals and halve the result.

Worked example

Find the area of the kite shown.

A = 12 × d₁ × d₂
= 12 × 12 × 9
= 12 × 108
= 54 cm²

Now you try

Find the area of a kite with diagonals 8 cm and 5 cm.

Show full solution
A = 12 × d₁ × d₂
= 12 × 8 × 5
= 12 × 40
= 20 cm²

Composite area

Split into familiar shapes or subtract a cut-out.

Worked example

10 by 7 cm rectangle with 3 by 2 cm removed.

A = 10 × 7 − 3 × 2
= 70 − 6
= 64 cm²

Now you try

9 by 6 cm rectangle with 2 by 1 cm removed.

Show full solution
A = 9 × 6 − 2 × 1
= 54 − 2
= 52 cm²

Circles

Diameter is twice radius. Circumference is πd; area is πr².

Worked example

Radius 4 cm: find circumference and area.

d = 2 × 4 = 8 cm
C = π × 8 = 8π cm
A = π × 4²
= 16π cm²

Now you try

Radius 5 cm: find circumference and area.

Show full solution
d = 2 × 5 = 10 cm
C = 10π cm
A = π × 5²
= 25π cm²

Volume and capacity units

Cube length conversion factors. 1 cm³ = 1 mL and 1000 mL = 1 L.

Worked example

Convert 3500 cm³ to litres.

3500 cm³ = 3500 mL
3500 ÷ 1000
= 3.5 L

Now you try

Convert 2 m³ to cm³.

Show full solution
1 m³ = 100³ cm³
= 1 000 000 cm³
2 × 1 000 000
= 2 000 000 cm³

Volume of prisms

Multiply cross-sectional area by prism length.

Worked example

Triangular prism: base 6 cm, height 4 cm, length 10 cm.

A = 12 × 6 × 4
= 12 cm²
V = A × length
= 12 × 10
= 120 cm³

Now you try

Rectangular prism: 7 by 4 by 3 cm.

Show full solution
V = 7 × 4 × 3
= 28 × 3
= 84 cm³

Multi-step measurement

Convert units first and check whether the answer needs length, area or volume units.

Worked example

Tank internal dimensions 40 by 25 by 30 cm. Find capacity in litres.

V = 40 × 25 × 30
= 1000 × 30
= 30 000 cm³
= 30 000 mL
= 30 L

Now you try

A tank has volume 2400 cm³ and base area 120 cm². Find height.

Show full solution
V = A × h
2400 = 120 × h
h = 2400 ÷ 120
= 20 cm
