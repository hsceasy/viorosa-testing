Key terms
budget
A plan for income and spending.
buy now, pay later
Purchase now and pay in instalments later.
deposit
Initial part-payment.
discretionary spending
Optional spending.
fixed spending
Regular predictable expenses.
goods and services tax (GST)
Generally 10% on taxable Australian sales.
loss
Cost price minus selling price when cost is higher.
model
Simplified mathematical representation of a real situation.
percentage decrease
Decrease divided by original amount, multiplied by 100%.
percentage increase
Increase divided by original amount, multiplied by 100%.
profit
Selling price minus cost price when selling price is higher.
stamp duty
State or territory tax on certain transactions.
value-added tax (VAT)
Consumption tax on goods and services; rates vary.
