Lesson 01

Length conversions

Convert lengths before calculating.

Worked example

Convert 2.4 m to cm.

2.4 × 100
= 240 cm

Now you try

Convert 3.6 m to cm.

Show worked solution

3.6 × 100
= 360 cm

Lesson 02

Area conversions

Square the length conversion factor.

Worked example

Convert 3 m² to cm².

1 m² = 100 × 100 cm²
= 10000 cm²
3 × 10000 = 30000 cm²

Now you try

Convert 2 m² to cm².

Show worked solution

2 × 100²
= 20000 cm²

Lesson 03

Composite perimeter

Count only the outside edges.

Worked example

A 10 by 8 cm rectangle has a 4 by 3 cm corner removed. Find perimeter.

P = 10 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 6 + 8
= 36 cm

Now you try

Find perimeter of a 12 by 9 cm rectangle with a 3 by 2 cm corner removed.

Show worked solution

P = 12 + 7 + 3 + 2 + 9 + 9
= 42 cm

Lesson 04

Composite area

Subtract the removed rectangle.

Worked example

Find area of a 10 by 8 cm rectangle with a 4 by 3 cm corner removed.

A = 10 × 8 − 4 × 3
= 80 − 12
= 68 cm²

Now you try

Find area of a 12 by 9 cm rectangle with a 3 by 2 cm corner removed.

Show worked solution

A = 12 × 9 − 3 × 2
= 108 − 6
= 102 cm²

Lesson 05

Triangle area

Use perpendicular height. A = F[b × h;2].

Worked example

Find area for base 8 cm and height 5 cm.

A = 8 × 52
= 402
= 20 cm²

Now you try

Find area for base 10 cm and height 6 cm.

Show worked solution

A = 10 × 62
= 602
= 30 cm²

Lesson 06

Trapezium area

Average the parallel sides and multiply by perpendicular height.

Worked example

Parallel sides 6 and 10 cm, height 4 cm. Find area.

A = 6 + 102 × 4
= 8 × 4
= 32 cm²

Now you try

Parallel sides 5 and 9 cm, height 6 cm. Find area.

Show worked solution

A = 5 + 92 × 6
= 7 × 6
= 42 cm²

Lesson 07

Volume conversions

Cube the length conversion factor.

Worked example

Convert 2 m³ to cm³.

2 × 100³
= 2 × 1000000
= 2000000 cm³

Now you try

Convert 3 m³ to cm³.

Show worked solution

3 × 100³
= 3000000 cm³

Lesson 08

Capacity

1 cm³ = 1 mL; 1000 mL = 1 L.

Worked example

Convert 4500 cm³ to litres.

4500 cm³ = 4500 mL
4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5 L

Now you try

Convert 3200 cm³ to litres.

Show worked solution

3200 ÷ 1000
= 3.2 L

Lesson 09

Rectangular prisms

Volume = length × width × height.

Worked example

Find volume of a 6 by 4 by 3 cm prism.

V = 6 × 4 × 3
= 24 × 3
= 72 cm³

Now you try

Find volume of a 8 by 5 by 2 cm prism.

Show worked solution

V = 8 × 5 × 2
= 40 × 2
= 80 cm³

Lesson 10

Triangular prisms

Volume = cross-sectional area × prism length.

Worked example

Triangle base 6 cm, height 4 cm, prism length 10 cm.

A = 6 × 42
= 12 cm²
V = 12 × 10
= 120 cm³

Now you try

Triangle base 8 cm, height 5 cm, prism length 6 cm.

Show worked solution

A = 8 × 52
= 20 cm²
V = 20 × 6
= 120 cm³

Lesson 11

Cylinders

Volume = πr²h.

Worked example

Find volume for radius 3 cm and height 8 cm.

V = π × 3² × 8
= π × 9 × 8
= 72π cm³

Now you try

Find volume for radius 4 cm and height 5 cm.

Show worked solution

V = π × 4² × 5
= 80π cm³

Lesson 12

Measurement applications

Convert to common units and round only at the end.

Worked example

A tank measures 50 by 40 by 30 cm. Find capacity.

V = 50 × 40 × 30
= 60000 cm³
= 60000 mL
= 60 L

Now you try

A tank measures 40 by 30 by 20 cm. Find capacity.

Show worked solution

V = 40 × 30 × 20
= 24000 cm³
= 24000 mL
= 24 L
