Key terms
adjacent side
side next to the chosen angle, excluding hypotenuse
alternate angle
equal angles on opposite sides of a transversal of parallel lines
ambiguous case of the sine rule
two possible triangles for some side-side-angle data
angle of depression
angle below horizontal
angle of elevation
angle above horizontal
bearing
direction from a reference point
compass bearing
direction measured from north or south toward east or west
cosine ratio
adjacent divided by hypotenuse
cosine rule
relates three sides and an included angle
degrees (geometry)
unit of angle; 360 in a full turn
hypotenuse
side opposite the right angle
minute (geometry)
one sixtieth of a degree
obtuse angle
angle greater than 90° and less than 180°
Pythagoras’ theorem
square of hypotenuse equals sum of squares of legs
radial survey
survey using distances and bearings from a station
sine ratio
opposite divided by hypotenuse
sine rule
relates sides to sines of opposite angles
tangent ratio
opposite divided by adjacent
trigonometric ratios
ratios of sides of right triangles
true bearing
clockwise angle from north, written with three digits
