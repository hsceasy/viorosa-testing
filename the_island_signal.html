🏝️ The Island Signal

A storm has cut communications across Beacon Island. Your rescue team has one chance to restore the signal and reach the evacuation helicopter. Each locked location hides a trigonometry problem. Solve it to unlock the next stage.

Mission briefing

14 locations. 14 locks. One rescue.

You'll tackle right-angled triangles, Pythagoras, angles of elevation and depression, true and compass bearings, sine and cosine rules, obtuse angles, the area rule and radial surveys.

Before you begin: Set your calculator to DEG mode. Every question tells you exactly how to round. Type your answer in the requested format, without units unless asked. Keep full precision during working and round only at the end.

Illustrations are schematic and not to scale. A calculator, pen and paper are recommended. Your progress is saved on this device.
