Lesson 01

Rational and irrational numbers

Rational numbers are ratios of integers with nonzero denominator. Irrational numbers have nonterminating, nonrecurring decimals.

Worked example

Classify √49 and √5.

√49 = 7 = 71
Rational; √5 is irrational.

Now you try

Classify √81.

Show worked solution

√81 = 9 = 91
Rational.

Lesson 02

Recurring decimals

Terminating and recurring decimals are rational.

Worked example

Convert 0.333… to a fraction.

x = 0.333…
10x = 3.333…
10x − x = 3.333… − 0.333…
9x = 3
x = 39 = 13

Now you try

Convert 0.777… to a fraction.

Show worked solution

x = 0.777…
10x = 7.777…
9x = 7
x = 79

Lesson 06

Decimal calculations

Align decimal places for addition; estimate to check products.

Worked example

Calculate 2.4 × 0.35.

24 × 35 = 840
2.4 × 0.35 = 8401000
= 0.84

Now you try

Calculate 1.2 × 0.45.

Show worked solution

12 × 45 = 540
5401000 = 0.54

Lesson 07

Conversions

A percentage is a fraction with denominator 100.

Worked example

Convert 78 to a percentage.

7 ÷ 8 = 0.875
0.875 × 100% = 87.5%

Now you try

Convert 38 to a percentage.

Show worked solution

3 ÷ 8 = 0.375
0.375 × 100% = 37.5%

Lesson 08

Percentage of a quantity

Convert the percentage to a decimal multiplier.

Worked example

Find 18% of $250.

18% = 0.18
0.18 × 250 = $45

Now you try

Find 12% of $350.

Show worked solution

12% = 0.12
0.12 × 350 = $42

Lesson 09

Percentage change

Divide the change by the original amount.

Worked example

A price rises from $80 to $100. Find the percentage increase.

Change = 100 − 80 = 20
2080 × 100%
= 25%

Now you try

A price falls from $120 to $90. Find the percentage decrease.

Show worked solution

Change = 120 − 90 = 30
30120 × 100%
= 25%

Lesson 10

Percentage multipliers

For an increase use 1 + rate; for a decrease use 1 − rate.

Worked example

Reduce $160 by 15%.

Multiplier = 1 − 0.15 = 0.85
160 × 0.85 = $136

Now you try

Increase $240 by 15%.

Show worked solution

Multiplier = 1 + 0.15 = 1.15
240 × 1.15 = $276

Lesson 11

Reverse percentages

Divide the final amount by its multiplier.

Worked example

After a 20% discount a jacket costs $72. Find original price.

0.8 × original = 72
original = 720.8
= $90

Now you try

After a 10% increase a price is $88. Find original price.

Show worked solution

1.1 × original = 88
original = 881.1
= $80

Lesson 12

GST and profit

GST is 10% in standard Australian examples. Profit percentage uses cost price.

Worked example

A $120 item excludes GST. Find inclusive price.

GST = 0.10 × 120 = 12
Total = 120 + 12 = $132

Now you try

An item costs $80 and sells for $100. Find profit percentage.

Show worked solution

Profit = 100 − 80 = 20
2080 × 100% = 25%
