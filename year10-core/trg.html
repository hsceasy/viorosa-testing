Lesson 01

Angles of elevation

Measure elevation upwards from the horizontal.

Worked example

A person looks up at a tower at 30°. Identify the angle.

The angle between the horizontal and upward sightline is 30°.

Now you try

A sightline is 40° above horizontal. Find elevation.

Show worked solution

40°

Lesson 02

Angles of depression

Measure depression downwards from the horizontal.

Worked example

A lookout sees a boat at 25° below horizontal.

Depression = 25°
The boat's elevation angle to the lookout is also 25° (alternate angles).

Now you try

Depression is 35°. Find corresponding elevation.

Show worked solution

35°

Lesson 03

Finding heights

Use tangent when horizontal distance and vertical height are involved.

Worked example

A tower is 20 m away; elevation is 30°. Find height.

tan 30° = h20
h = 20 tan 30°
h ≈ 11.55 m

Now you try

Distance 10 m, elevation 45°. Find height.

Show worked solution

h = 10 tan 45° = 10 m

Lesson 04

Finding distances

Rearrange the tangent ratio to find horizontal distance.

Worked example

A 12 m cliff has depression angle 30° to a boat.

tan 30° = 12d
d = 12tan 30°
d ≈ 20.78 m

Now you try

Height 8 m, depression 45°. Find distance.

Show worked solution

d = 8tan 45° = 8 m

Lesson 05

Finding angles

Use inverse tangent to find an angle from opposite and adjacent sides.

Worked example

Height 6 m, horizontal distance 8 m.

tan θ = 68
θ = tan⁻¹(0.75)
θ ≈ 36.87°

Now you try

Height and distance both 9 m. Find elevation.

Show worked solution

θ = tan⁻¹(1) = 45°

Lesson 06

Eye height

Add observer eye height to the vertical rise above eye level.

Worked example

Eye height 1.5 m, distance 10 m, elevation 45°. Find tower height.

Rise = 10 tan 45° = 10 m
Total = 10 + 1.5 = 11.5 m

Now you try

Eye height 1.6 m, distance 8 m, elevation 45°.

Show worked solution

8 + 1.6 = 9.6 m

Lesson 07

True bearings

Measure clockwise from north using three digits.

Worked example

Express due east as a true bearing.

Clockwise quarter turn = 090°.

Now you try

Express due south as a true bearing.

Show worked solution

180°

Lesson 08

Compass bearings

Start from north or south and turn towards east or west.

Worked example

Describe N30°E as a true bearing.

30° clockwise from north = 030°.

Now you try

Convert S20°E to true bearing.

Show worked solution

180° − 20° = 160°

Lesson 09

Converting bearings

Use the quadrant to convert between bearing systems.

Worked example

Convert 240° to a compass bearing.

240° − 180° = 60°
S60°W

Now you try

Convert 310° to a compass bearing.

Show worked solution

360° − 310° = 50°
N50°W

Lesson 10

Reverse bearings

Reverse direction by adding or subtracting 180°.

Worked example

A to B has bearing 070°. Find B to A.

70° + 180° = 250°.

Now you try

Reverse 220°.

Show worked solution

220° − 180° = 040°

Lesson 11

Bearing components

For a bearing in the northeast quadrant, north displacement uses cosine and east displacement uses sine.

Worked example

Travel 10 km on bearing 030°. Find east displacement.

East = 10 sin 30° = 5 km

Now you try

Travel 12 km on bearing 060°. Find north displacement.

Show worked solution

North = 12 cos 60° = 6 km

Lesson 12

Navigation problems

Draw a north arrow at each location and use right triangle ratios.

Worked example

Travel 3 km east then 4 km north. Find direct distance and bearing.

Distance = √(3² + 4²) = 5 km
θ = tan⁻¹(34) ≈ 36.87°
Bearing ≈ 037°

Now you try

Travel 5 km east then 5 km north. Find bearing.

Show worked solution

θ = tan⁻¹(1) = 45°
Bearing = 045°
