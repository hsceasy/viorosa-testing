Lesson 01

Uniform cross-sections

Volume of a right prism equals cross-sectional area multiplied by perpendicular length.

Worked example

A rectangular cross-section is 4 cm by 3 cm and length is 8 cm. Find volume.

A = 4 × 3 = 12 cm²
V = 12 × 8 = 96 cm³

Now you try

A prism has cross-sectional area 15 cm² and length 6 cm. Find volume.

Show worked solution

V = 15 × 6 = 90 cm³

Lesson 02

Triangular prisms

Find the triangular area using half the base times perpendicular height.

Worked example

A triangular cross-section has base 6 cm and height 4 cm. Length is 10 cm.

A = 12 × 6 × 4 = 12 cm²
V = 12 × 10 = 120 cm³

Now you try

Base 8 cm, height 5 cm, length 7 cm. Find volume.

Show worked solution

A = 12 × 8 × 5 = 20 cm²
V = 20 × 7 = 140 cm³

Lesson 03

Trapezoidal prisms

Use half the sum of parallel sides times their perpendicular separation.

Worked example

Parallel sides 4 and 8 cm, separation 3 cm, prism length 5 cm.

A = 12 × (4 + 8) × 3 = 18 cm²
V = 18 × 5 = 90 cm³

Now you try

Parallel sides 5 and 9 cm, separation 4 cm, length 6 cm.

Show worked solution

A = 12 × 14 × 4 = 28 cm²
V = 28 × 6 = 168 cm³

Lesson 04

L-shaped prisms

Subtract a missing rectangle from the enclosing rectangle before multiplying by length.

Worked example

An 8 by 6 cm cross-section has a 3 by 2 cm corner removed. Length 10 cm.

A = 8 × 6 − 3 × 2 = 42 cm²
V = 42 × 10 = 420 cm³

Now you try

A 9 by 7 cm section has a 2 by 3 cm corner removed. Length 4 cm.

Show worked solution

A = 63 − 6 = 57 cm²
V = 57 × 4 = 228 cm³

Lesson 05

House-shaped prisms

Dissect a composite cross-section into a rectangle and triangle.

Worked example

A 6 by 4 cm rectangle supports a triangle of base 6 cm and height 2 cm. Length 9 cm.

A = 6 × 4 + 12 × 6 × 2 = 30 cm²
V = 30 × 9 = 270 cm³

Now you try

Rectangle 8 by 3 cm, triangle base 8 cm and height 2 cm, length 5 cm.

Show worked solution

A = 24 + 8 = 32 cm²
V = 32 × 5 = 160 cm³

Lesson 06

Cylinders

A cylinder has a circular cross-section. Use V = πr²h.

Worked example

Radius 3 cm and height 7 cm. Find exact volume.

V = π × 3² × 7
= 63π cm³

Now you try

Radius 4 cm and height 5 cm. Find exact volume.

Show worked solution

V = π × 16 × 5 = 80π cm³

Lesson 07

Semicircular prisms

A semicircle has half the area of a circle.

Worked example

Radius 4 cm and length 6 cm. Find exact volume.

A = 12 × π × 4² = 8π cm²
V = 48π cm³

Now you try

Radius 6 cm, length 5 cm. Find exact volume.

Show worked solution

A = 18π cm²
V = 90π cm³

Lesson 08

Quadrant prisms

A quadrant is one quarter of a circle.

Worked example

Radius 8 cm and length 3 cm. Find exact volume.

A = 14 × π × 64 = 16π cm²
V = 48π cm³

Now you try

Radius 4 cm and length 9 cm. Find exact volume.

Show worked solution

A = 4π cm²
V = 36π cm³

Lesson 09

Sector prisms

Multiply circle area by the fraction of a full turn.

Worked example

A 60° sector has radius 6 cm and prism length 5 cm.

A = 60360 × π × 36 = 6π cm²
V = 30π cm³

Now you try

A 120° sector has radius 3 cm and length 8 cm.

Show worked solution

A = 120360 × π × 9 = 3π cm²
V = 24π cm³

Lesson 10

Composite solids

Add volumes of non-overlapping component solids.

Worked example

A 6 by 6 by 2 cm block supports a cylinder of radius 2 cm and height 5 cm.

Block = 72 cm³
Cylinder = 20π cm³
Total = 72 + 20π cm³

Now you try

A 4 by 4 by 3 cm block supports a cylinder of radius 1 cm and height 4 cm.

Show worked solution

Total = 48 + 4π cm³

Lesson 11

Hollow solids

Subtract the volume of the removed portion.

Worked example

A cylinder of radius 5 cm and height 10 cm has a coaxial hole of radius 2 cm.

V = π × (25 − 4) × 10
= 210π cm³

Now you try

Outer radius 4 cm, inner radius 2 cm, length 6 cm.

Show worked solution

V = π × (16 − 4) × 6 = 72π cm³

Lesson 12

Capacity and practical problems

Use 1 cm³ = 1 mL and 1000 cm³ = 1 L. Use internal dimensions for capacity.

Worked example

A tank has an internal 50 by 40 cm base and water depth 30 cm.

V = 50 × 40 × 30 = 60 000 cm³
Capacity at this depth = 60 L

Now you try

A tank has internal base 60 by 40 cm and water depth 25 cm.

Show worked solution

V = 60 000 cm³ = 60 L
