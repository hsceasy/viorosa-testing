Your investigation

Use the graphing panel to compare y=ax² and y=x²+c. Change a from 1 to 2 to −1; then compare c=0, 2 and −2. Describe the opening, width and vertex.

Check your reasoning

Larger |a| narrows the parabola; negative a opens down. Changing c translates the vertex vertically to (0,c).
