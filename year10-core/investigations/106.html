Constructing prism nets

Back to learning hub · All investigations

Your investigation

On squared paper draw six rectangles for a 4 cm × 3 cm × 2 cm rectangular prism: two of each size. Arrange them into a connected net, cut and fold to test it. Rearrange into a different net and test again. Sketch both successful nets.

Check your reasoning

Two 4×3, two 4×2 and two 3×2 faces. One valid arrangement is a strip of four side faces (4×2, 3×2, 4×2, 3×2) joined along 2 cm edges, with the 4×3 faces attached to opposite long edges of one 4×2 face. Other foldable nets are valid.
