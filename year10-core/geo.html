Lesson 01

Similar figures

Similar figures have equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides.

Worked example

Rectangles 3 by 4 and 6 by 8: are they similar?

6 ÷ 3 = 2
8 ÷ 4 = 2
All angles are 90°, so yes.

Now you try

Are 2 by 5 and 4 by 10 rectangles similar?

Show worked solution

4 ÷ 2 = 10 ÷ 5 = 2, yes.

Lesson 02

Corresponding vertices

Match vertices in the same relative positions, even after rotation.

Worked example

If ABC is similar to DEF, which angle matches B?

The second vertex is E.
Angle B = angle E.

Now you try

Which side matches AC?

Show worked solution

DF

Lesson 03

Corresponding angles

Corresponding angles in similar figures are equal.

Worked example

Angle A is 50° and matches D. Find D.

D = A = 50°.

Now you try

Angle B = 70°. Find corresponding E.

Show worked solution

E = 70°

Lesson 04

Enlargements

Scale factor is image length divided by original length.

Worked example

A 4 cm side becomes 10 cm.

k = 104 = 2.5

Now you try

A 6 cm side becomes 15 cm. Find k.

Show worked solution

k = 156 = 2.5

Lesson 05

Reductions

A scale factor between zero and one produces a reduction.

Worked example

A 12 cm side becomes 3 cm.

k = 312 = 14

Now you try

A 20 cm side becomes 5 cm. Find k.

Show worked solution

k = 520 = 0.25

Lesson 06

Unknown sides

Multiply the original length by the scale factor.

Worked example

A triangle has sides 3,4,5 cm. Enlarge by 2.

3 × 2 = 6
4 × 2 = 8
5 × 2 = 10 cm

Now you try

Enlarge a 7 cm side by 3.

Show worked solution

7 × 3 = 21 cm

Lesson 07

Proportion

Equate ratios of corresponding sides.

Worked example

Similar triangles have matching sides 3 and 6, 5 and x.

36 = 5x
3x = 30
x = 10

Now you try

Matching sides 4 and 12, 5 and x. Find x.

Show worked solution

4x = 60
x = 15

Lesson 08

Similar circles

All circles are similar; compare radii or diameters.

Worked example

Radii 2 cm and 5 cm. Find scale factor.

k = 52 = 2.5

Now you try

Diameters 8 cm and 12 cm. Find k.

Show worked solution

k = 128 = 1.5

Lesson 09

Map scales

Convert measurements to the same units before using a scale.

Worked example

On a 1:50 000 map, 4 cm represents what distance?

4 × 50 000 = 200 000 cm
= 2 km

Now you try

Find actual distance for 6 cm at 1:50 000.

Show worked solution

300 000 cm = 3 km

Lesson 10

Plans

A plan represents actual lengths by a constant ratio.

Worked example

A room is 5 m long. Draw at 1:100.

5 m = 500 cm
500 ÷ 100 = 5 cm

Now you try

Draw a 4 m wall at 1:50.

Show worked solution

400 ÷ 50 = 8 cm

Lesson 11

Photographs

Enlarging a photograph preserves its aspect ratio.

Worked example

A 10 by 15 cm photo is enlarged to width 20 cm.

k = 20 ÷ 10 = 2
Height = 15 × 2 = 30 cm

Now you try

A 6 by 9 photo becomes 12 cm wide. Find height.

Show worked solution

k = 2
9 × 2 = 18 cm

Lesson 12

Mixed scale problems

Use a consistent scale factor for all corresponding lengths.

Worked example

A model car is 12 cm long at 1:25.

12 × 25 = 300 cm = 3 m

Now you try

A 16 cm model at 1:25 represents what length?

Show worked solution

16 × 25 = 400 cm = 4 m
