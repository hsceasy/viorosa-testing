Lesson 01

Expressions and equations

An equation states that two expressions are equal. A solution makes the equality true.

Worked example

Write an equation for three more than a number being 12.

Let x be the number.
x + 3 = 12

Now you try

Write an equation for twice a number being 18.

Show worked solution

Let x be the number.
2x = 18

Lesson 02

One-step addition and subtraction

Keep an equation balanced by performing the same operation on both sides.

Worked example

Solve x − 7 = 12.

x − 7 = 12
x − 7 + 7 = 12 + 7
x = 19

Now you try

Solve x + 8 = 15.

Show worked solution

x + 8 = 15
x + 8 − 8 = 15 − 8
x = 7

Lesson 03

One-step multiplication and division

Undo multiplication by division, and division by multiplication.

Worked example

Solve 5x = 35.

5x = 35
5x5 = 355
x = 7

Now you try

Solve x4 = 6.

Show worked solution

x4 = 6
4 × x4 = 4 × 6
x = 24

Lesson 04

Two-step equations

Undo addition or subtraction before undoing multiplication.

Worked example

Solve 3x + 5 = 20.

3x + 5 = 20
3x + 5 − 5 = 20 − 5
3x = 15
3x3 = 153
x = 5

Now you try

Solve 4x − 7 = 21.

Show worked solution

4x − 7 = 21
4x − 7 + 7 = 21 + 7
4x = 28
4x4 = 284
x = 7

Lesson 05

Negative coefficients

Divide by the negative coefficient, retaining its sign.

Worked example

Solve −3x + 4 = 19.

−3x + 4 = 19
−3x + 4 − 4 = 19 − 4
−3x = 15
−3x−3 = 15−3
x = −5

Now you try

Solve −2x − 3 = 9.

Show worked solution

−2x − 3 = 9
−2x − 3 + 3 = 9 + 3
−2x = 12
−2x−2 = 12−2
x = −6

Lesson 06

Fractional equations

Multiply both sides by the denominator to clear it.

Worked example

Solve x + 23 = 5.

x + 23 = 5
3 × x + 23 = 3 × 5
x + 2 = 15
x + 2 − 2 = 15 − 2
x = 13

Now you try

Solve x − 45 = 3.

Show worked solution

x − 45 = 3
5 × x − 45 = 5 × 3
x − 4 = 15
x − 4 + 4 = 15 + 4
x = 19

Lesson 07

Non-integer solutions

Keep exact fractions until a decimal is needed.

Worked example

Solve 4x + 1 = 8.

4x + 1 = 8
4x + 1 − 1 = 8 − 1
4x = 7
4x4 = 74
x = 74
= 1.75

Now you try

Solve 5x − 2 = 11.

Show worked solution

5x − 2 = 11
5x − 2 + 2 = 11 + 2
5x = 13
5x5 = 135
x = 135
= 2.6

Lesson 08

Unknown on both sides

Collect variable terms on one side by subtracting the same term from both sides.

Worked example

Solve 5x = 2x + 12.

5x = 2x + 12
5x − 2x = 2x + 12 − 2x
3x = 12
3x3 = 123
x = 4

Now you try

Solve 7x = 3x + 20.

Show worked solution

7x = 3x + 20
7x − 3x = 3x + 20 − 3x
4x = 20
4x4 = 204
x = 5

Lesson 09

Word problems

Define the unknown, form an equation, solve and interpret the answer.

Worked example

Three tickets and a $4 fee cost $34. Find one ticket price.

Let x be the ticket price in dollars.
3x + 4 = 34
3x + 4 − 4 = 34 − 4
3x = 30
3x3 = 303
x = 10
One ticket costs $10.

Now you try

Two books and $5 postage cost $29. Find one book price.

Show worked solution

Let x be the book price.
2x + 5 = 29
2x + 5 − 5 = 29 − 5
2x = 24
2x2 = 242
x = 12

Lesson 10

Verification by substitution

Substitute into the original equation and compare both sides.

Worked example

Check x = 4 in 3x + 2 = 14.

LHS = 3(4) + 2
= 12 + 2
= 14
RHS = 14
LHS = RHS, so x = 4 is correct.

Now you try

Check x = 5 in 2x − 1 = 9.

Show worked solution

LHS = 2(5) − 1
= 10 − 1
= 9
RHS = 9
LHS = RHS.

Lesson 11

Substitution into formulas

Substitute known values before solving for the unknown.

Worked example

A = bh. A = 42 and b = 7. Find h.

42 = 7h
427 = 7h7
6 = h
h = 6

Now you try

P = 2l + 2w. P = 30 and l = 9. Find w.

Show worked solution

30 = 2(9) + 2w
30 = 18 + 2w
30 − 18 = 18 + 2w − 18
12 = 2w
122 = 2w2
6 = w
w = 6

Lesson 12

Quadratic equations

For x² = a with a positive, both the positive and negative square roots are solutions.

Worked example

Solve x² = 20 exactly and approximately.

x = ±√20
x = ±2√5
x ≈ ±4.47
Check: (±2√5)² = 4 × 5 = 20.

Now you try

Solve x² = 49.

Show worked solution

x = ±√49
x = ±7
Check: 7² = 49 and (−7)² = 49.
