Activity networks

Critical Path Analysis course notes

Key concept

Arrows represent activities with durations in an activity-on-arrow network.

A dummy activity has duration zero.

Worked example 1

A then B take 4 and 6 days. Find duration.

4+6=10 days.

Worked example 2

A and B start together and take 4 and 6 days. When are both finished?

After 6 days.

Common mistake

Parallel activities need not be added.
