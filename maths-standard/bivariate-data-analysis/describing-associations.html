Describing associations

Bivariate Data Analysis course notes

Key concepts

Describe form, direction and strength. Strong linear associations cluster around a straight line; curved patterns are non-linear.

Form · direction · strength

Worked example

Describe the association between advertising and orders.
Worked solution
  1. The points follow an approximately straight rising pattern.
  2. The association is strong, positive and approximately linear.

Now you try

Describe the association between age and value.
Show answer
  1. The points follow a falling straight-line pattern.
  2. Strong negative linear association.
