Network concepts and applications

A network represents objects (vertices) and their connections (edges). In a social network, vertices are people and edges are friendships; in traffic networks, vertices are intersections and directed edges represent one-way roads. Supply chains connect suppliers and warehouses; communication networks connect devices or towers.

Vertex A has degree 3 because AB, AC and AD meet there. The weight on AB is 2; weights may represent time, cost or distance, not the drawn length.
