VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 11 · MATHEMATICS STANDARD

Walks trails paths and circuits

Key idea

A walk may repeat edges and vertices; a trail does not repeat edges; a path does not repeat vertices; a circuit is a closed trail.

Worked example 1

In triangle ABC, A–B–C is a path.

Worked example 2

A–B–C–A is a circuit.

Common mistake: Calling every walk a path.
