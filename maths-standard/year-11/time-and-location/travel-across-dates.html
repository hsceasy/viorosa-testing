UTC and international time differences

Key idea

Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) is the reference for time zones. Positive offsets are ahead; negative offsets are behind.

Convert to UTC

UTC = local time − local offset

Convert from UTC

Destination time = UTC + destination offset

International meeting

City A (UTC−5) schedules a call at 18:00 Monday. City B is UTC+8.

Difference = 8 − (−5) = 13 h

18:00 Monday + 13 h = 07:00 Tuesday.

Flight arrival

A flight leaves a city at UTC+10 at 21:30 Friday, flies 9 h and lands at UTC−7. Find local arrival.

Departure UTC = 21:30 − 10 h = 11:30 Friday

Arrival UTC = 11:30 + 9 h = 20:30 Friday

Arrival local = 20:30 − 7 h = 13:30 Friday.

Previous date

At UTC+8 it is 02:00 Tuesday. Find time at UTC−5.

UTC = 02:00 Tuesday − 8 h = 18:00 Monday

Destination = 18:00 Monday − 5 h = 13:00 Monday.
