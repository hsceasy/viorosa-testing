Australian time zones and daylight saving

Key idea

Daylight saving advances clocks by one hour in participating states during the warmer months. Queensland, NT and WA do not observe it. Use the date and offsets given in the question.

LocationStandard UTCDaylight saving UTC
Perth+8+8
Darwin+9:30+9:30
Adelaide+9:30+10:30
Brisbane+10+10
Sydney, Melbourne, Hobart, Canberra+10+11

These are the main capital-city zones; some regional and island exceptions exist.

Summer meeting

In January, a meeting starts at 14:00 in Sydney. Find the time in Adelaide and Perth.

Sydney UTC+11; Adelaide UTC+10:30; Perth UTC+8.

Adelaide difference = 10 h 30 min − 11 h = −30 min

Adelaide = 13:30

Perth difference = 8 − 11 = −3 h

Perth = 11:00

Winter comparison

In July, Sydney is UTC+10 and Brisbane UTC+10. Sydney time is 09:15.

Difference = 10 − 10 = 0 h

Brisbane = 09:15.
