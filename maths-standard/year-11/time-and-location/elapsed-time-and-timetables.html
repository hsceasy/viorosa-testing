Elapsed time and timetables

Key idea

Elapsed time is the duration between two times. Count to a convenient hour or midnight, then add the remaining minutes.

Crossing midnight

A coach leaves at 22:45 and arrives at 01:20 the next day.

22:45 to 00:00 = 1 h 15 min

00:00 to 01:20 = 1 h 20 min

Total = 2 h 35 min

Timetable

Find the transfer time and total journey duration.

Transfer = 09:27 − 09:18 = 9 min

First trip = 36 min

Second trip = 37 min

Total = 36 + 9 + 37 = 82 min = 1 h 22 min.
