Time conversions and formats

Key idea

Time units

1 h = 60 min; 1 min = 60 s; 1 h = 3600 s

Multiply when converting to smaller units; divide when converting to larger units. In 24-hour time, add 12 to afternoon hours 1–11. Midnight is 00:00 and noon is 12:00.

Decimal hours

Convert 2.35 hours to hours, minutes and seconds.

2.35 h = 2 h + 0.35 h

0.35 × 60 = 21 min

Answer: 2 h 21 min = 2 h 21 min 0 s.

Seconds to hours

Convert 5400 seconds to hours.

5400 ÷ 60 = 90 min

90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 h

Clock formats

Convert 8:45 pm and 00:15.

8 + 12 = 20, so 8:45 pm = 20:45.

00:15 = 12:15 am.
