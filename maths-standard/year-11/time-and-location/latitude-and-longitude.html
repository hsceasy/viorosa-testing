Latitude, longitude and position coordinates

Key idea

Latitude measures angular distance north or south of the equator (0°), up to 90°. Longitude measures angular distance east or west of the prime meridian (0°), up to 180°. Write latitude first, longitude second.

Horizontal grid lines show latitude; vertical grid lines show longitude on this simplified map. On a globe, meridians meet at the poles.

Reading a map

Identify P on the grid.

Latitude = 20° S

Longitude = 30° E

Position = (20° S, 30° E).

Using a digital map

Locate a point with coordinates (−33.87, 151.21).

Negative latitude means south; positive longitude means east.

Enter −33.87, 151.21 into a digital map search box.

The point is near central Sydney. Compare the marker with the equator and prime meridian.
