VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 11

Privacy, ethics and bias

Learn the concept

Collect only necessary information, seek informed consent, protect identities and report honestly. Offer accessible formats, translations where appropriate and culturally respectful questions. Avoid assumptions about diverse communities; consult relevant groups.

Worked example

A health survey asks for names and diagnoses in a public form. Remove unnecessary identifiers, explain voluntary participation, restrict access and publish aggregate results. Small groups may still be identifiable.

Now you try

Why might an English-only online survey be biased?

Show worked answer

It may exclude people without internet access or sufficient English proficiency; offer accessible alternatives.
