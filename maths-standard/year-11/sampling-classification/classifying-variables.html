VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 11

Classifying and interpreting variables

Learn the concept

The meaning of a response matters more than its appearance. Numeric codes for categories do not necessarily make the variable numerical.

Worked example

Fan settings 1, 2, 3 correspond to low, medium, high: ordinal. Actual fan speed in revolutions per minute is numerical. Quality ratings 1–5 are ordered categories unless a justified measurement scale is defined.

Now you try

Compare jersey number and number of goals.

Show worked answer

Jersey number is nominal; goals are discrete numerical.
