Populations and samples

Learn the concept

A population is the entire group of interest; a sample is a subset. A census attempts to collect information from every member. A sampling frame is the list used to select participants.

Worked example

A council has 12,000 residents and surveys 300 randomly selected residents. Population = 12,000; sample = 300 respondents if all respond; sampling fraction = 2.5%. A census costs more and may still have non-response.

Now you try

A school surveys 80 of 800 students. Find the sampling fraction.

Show worked answer

80 ÷ 800 × 100 = 10%.
