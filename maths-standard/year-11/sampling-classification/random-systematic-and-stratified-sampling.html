Sampling methods

Learn the concept

Simple random: every possible sample of the chosen size has equal chance. Systematic: random start then every kth member. Stratified: randomly sample within groups, often proportionally. Self-selected: volunteers choose to respond; prone to bias.

MethodSelection example

Worked example

For 600 students (240 junior, 360 senior), select 50: junior 240/600 × 50 = 20; senior 30. Systematic sampling uses interval 600/50 = 12, e.g. start 7 then 19, 31, …, 595. A repeating pattern in the list can bias systematic sampling.
