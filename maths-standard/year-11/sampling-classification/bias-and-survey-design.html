Representativeness and collection errors

Learn the concept

Coverage, non-response, leading questions, measurement error and volunteer bias can distort results. Experiments require fair comparison and control of confounding variables; observational studies cannot alone establish causation. Media reports may misrepresent a sample as a population.

Worked example

A gym surveys 200 members at 6 am and claims all residents exercise daily. The sampling frame excludes non-members and other times; a larger sample does not fix this. Randomly sample residents across locations and times.

Now you try

A study finds students who tutor score higher. Can tutoring be declared the cause?

Show worked answer

No. Prior attainment and motivation may confound the observational association.
