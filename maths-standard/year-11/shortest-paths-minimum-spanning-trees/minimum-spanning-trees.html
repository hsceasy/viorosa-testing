Minimum spanning trees and minimal connectors

A tree with n vertices has n − 1 edges. If the three edges above are undirected, AB and BC form a minimum spanning tree of weight 5. A shortest path connects two specified vertices; a spanning tree connects all vertices.

Continue to the Shortest Paths and Minimum Spanning Trees hub for further practice.

Kruskal’s method: order edges by weight (2, 3, 4, 5, 6). Select AB, BC and CD, skipping any edge that would create a cycle. Stop after three edges. Prim’s method: start at A, choose AB, then BC, then CD, always choosing the cheapest edge from the connected group to a new vertex.

The minimum total cable length is 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 m. At $12 per metre, the school pays $108. The same approach can connect a power station and substations while minimising cable length.
