Interpolation and extrapolation

Key idea

Interpolation means estimating between values you already know. Extrapolation means estimating beyond the values you know. Think of filling a gap versus extending a pattern.

Interpolation — inside the known range

A tank contains 4 L after 1 minute and 10 L after 4 minutes. Assume a constant filling rate. Estimate the volume after 2 minutes.

Solution

m = 10 − 44 − 1

m = 63

m = 2

V = 2t + c

4 = 2(1) + c

4 = 2 + c

4 − 2 = c

c = 2

V = 2t + 2

V = 2(2) + 2

V = 4 + 2

V = 6

6 L. Two minutes is between 1 and 4 minutes.

Extrapolation — outside the known range

Using the same model, estimate the volume after 5 minutes.

Solution

V = 2t + 2

V = 2(5) + 2

V = 10 + 2

V = 12

12 L, if the rate stays constant and the tank has enough capacity.

Extrapolation is generally less reliable because the pattern may change beyond the observed data.
