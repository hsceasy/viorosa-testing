Wages and overtime

Key idea

Ordinary pay

Hours × hourly rate

Overtime pay

Overtime hours × ordinary rate × multiplier

Time-and-a-half means 1.5 times the ordinary rate; double time means twice the rate.

Calculate gross pay

Mia works 38 ordinary hours at $28 per hour and 4 hours at time-and-a-half. Find gross pay.

Ordinary pay = 38 × 28

= $1,064

Overtime rate = 28 × 1.5

= $42

Overtime pay = 4 × 42

= $168

Gross pay = 1,064 + 168

= $1,232

Find the ordinary rate

Noah earns $1,380 for 38 ordinary hours and 8 hours at time-and-a-half. Find his ordinary hourly rate.

Let r be the ordinary hourly rate.

38r + 8(1.5r) = 1,380

38r + 12r = 1,380

50r = 1,380

r = 1,38050

r = $27.60 per hour
