Leave loading and superannuation

Key idea

  1. Calculate ordinary pay for the leave period.
  2. Convert the loading percentage to a decimal.
  3. Multiply ordinary leave pay by the loading rate.
  4. Add the loading to ordinary leave pay.

Leave loading

Ordinary leave pay × loading rate

Superannuation

Eligible earnings × contribution rate

Annual leave

A worker earns $1,240 weekly and takes three weeks of leave with 17.5% loading. Find total leave pay.

Ordinary leave pay = 3 × 1,240

= $3,720

Loading = 0.175 × 3,720

= $651

Total = 3,720 + 651

= $4,371

Superannuation

An employer contributes 12% of eligible ordinary earnings of $2,500 per fortnight. Find the contribution.

Contribution = 0.12 × 2,500

= $300
