Taxable income and tax brackets

Key idea

Use this Australian resident income tax table for 2024–25 and 2025–26 classroom examples. Rates exclude Medicare levy. Ignore offsets and other adjustments.

Taxable incomeIncome tax payable
$0–18,200Nil
$18,201–45,00016% of amount above $18,200
$45,001–135,000$4,288 + 30% of amount above $45,000
$135,001–190,000$31,288 + 37% of amount above $135,000
$190,001+$51,638 + 45% of amount above $190,000

Taxable income

Assessable income − allowable deductions

Medicare levy

Taxable income × 0.02

Assume the full 2% levy applies; real reductions and exemptions may apply.

Four-part tax calculation

A worker earns $72,000 and has $2,000 allowable deductions. Find (a) taxable income, (b) bracket, (c) income tax and (d) Medicare levy.

(a) Taxable income = 72,000 − 2,000

= $70,000

(b) $45,001–$135,000 bracket.

(c) Tax = 4,288 + 0.30(70,000 − 45,000)

= 4,288 + 0.30 × 25,000

= 4,288 + 7,500

= $11,788

(d) Levy = 0.02 × 70,000

= $1,400

Total tax and levy = 11,788 + 1,400

= $13,188
