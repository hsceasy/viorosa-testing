Pythagoras’ theorem

Key idea

Only for right-angled triangles. c is the hypotenuse, opposite the right angle.

Pythagoras

c² = a² + b²

Ladder

A ladder reaches 4 m up a wall and its foot is 3 m away. Find its length.

c² = 3² + 4²

c² = 9 + 16

c² = 25

c = √25

c = 5 m

Find a shorter side

A right triangle has hypotenuse 13 cm and one side 5 cm. Find the other side.

13² = 5² + b²

169 = 25 + b²

144 = b²

b = √144

b = 12 cm
