Volume, Capacity and Surface Area

Key idea

Volume measures space occupied; capacity is internal volume. 1 cm³ = 1 mL, 1000 cm³ = 1 L, 1 m³ = 1000 L.

Prism volume

V = cross-sectional area × length

Cylinder volume

V = πr²h

Closed cylinder surface area

SA = 2πrh + 2πr²

Open at one end

SA = 2πrh + πr²

Open at both ends

SA = 2πrh

These surface areas assume negligible wall thickness.

Water tank

A cylindrical tank has internal radius 20 cm and height 50 cm. Find capacity.

V = π × 20² × 50

V = 20 000π cm³

Capacity = 20 000π ÷ 1000

Capacity = 20π ≈ 62.83 L

Open container

A cylinder of radius 4 cm and height 10 cm has no lid. Find surface area.

SA = 2πrh + πr²

SA = 2π × 4 × 10 + π × 4²

SA = 80π + 16π

SA = 96π ≈ 301.59 cm²

Triangular prism

A prism has triangular cross-section with base 6 cm, perpendicular height 4 cm and length 12 cm.

A = 6 × 42

A = 12 cm²

V = 12 × 12

V = 144 cm³
