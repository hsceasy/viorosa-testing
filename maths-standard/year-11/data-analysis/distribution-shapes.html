Distribution shapes and modality

Learn the concept

Symmetric distributions have similar halves. Positive skew has a longer right tail; negative skew has a longer left tail. Uniform has approximately equal frequencies; unimodal has one peak, bimodal two, multimodal more than two.

Worked example

Waiting times often have positive skew because a few unusually long waits extend the right tail. Two peaks may reflect two different groups; investigate rather than assume.

Now you try

A test has most scores near 90 with a few very low scores. Describe its skew.

Show worked answer

Negative (left) skew.
