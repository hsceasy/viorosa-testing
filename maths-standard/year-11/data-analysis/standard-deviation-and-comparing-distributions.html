Measures of spread and standard deviation

Learn the concept

Range = maximum − minimum. Population standard deviation σ measures typical spread around the mean: square deviations, average them over N, then take the square root. Use σx, not sample sx, on a calculator.

Worked example

For 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, mean = 6; squared deviations 16, 4, 0, 4, 16 sum to 40. σ = √(40/5) = √8 ≈ 2.83; range = 8.

Now you try

Find σ for 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

Show worked answer

Mean 6; σ = √(10/5) ≈ 1.41; range 4.
