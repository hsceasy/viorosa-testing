VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 11

Comparing datasets

Learn the concept

Compare centre and spread together, in context and with units. A lower spread indicates greater consistency, not necessarily better outcomes. Compare like populations and measurement methods.

Worked example

Bus A times 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 and B times 10, 11, 12, 13, 14 both average 12 minutes. A has range 8 and σ≈2.83; B range 4 and σ≈1.41. B is more predictable.

Now you try

Which bus is more consistent? Can you conclude it is always faster?

Show worked answer

B is more consistent; neither is always faster.
