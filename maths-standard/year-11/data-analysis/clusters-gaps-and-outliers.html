Clusters, gaps and outliers

Learn the concept

Clusters are concentrations, gaps are empty intervals. Outlier fences are Q1 − 1.5 × IQR and Q3 + 1.5 × IQR. Values strictly outside are outliers; investigate rather than automatically delete them.

Worked example

Data 2, 3, 4, 5, 5, 5, 6, 7, 8, 20: Q1=4, Q3=7, IQR=3, fences −0.5 and 11.5. Thus 20 is an outlier. Mean rises from 5 to 6.5, median stays 5, range rises from 6 to 18, IQR stays 3 (the quartile positions shift). A long delay may be genuine.
