Cumulative frequency and polygons

Learn the concept

Cumulative frequency is the running total. Plot at upper class boundaries, starting at the lower boundary with zero. Read approximate quartiles at N/4, N/2 and 3N/4. Cumulative frequency histograms use running totals as bar heights.

Worked example

Counts 2, 6, 8, 4 produce totals 2, 8, 16, 20. At 20 minutes the cumulative count is 8. Median position 10 lies between 20 and 30; linear interpolation gives 22.5 minutes (estimate).

Now you try

Estimate Q3.

Show worked answer

Position 15: 20 + (15−8)/8 × 10 = 28.75 minutes.
