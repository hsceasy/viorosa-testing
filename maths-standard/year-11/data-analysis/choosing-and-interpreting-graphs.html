VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 11

Choosing and interpreting graphs

Learn the concept

Use dot plots or stem-and-leaf plots for small numerical datasets, histograms for grouped measurements, line graphs for time trends, and sector or divided bar charts for proportions. Choose a column graph for category comparisons.

StemGroup A leavesGroup B leaves
12 5 83 6
20 41 2 7

Stem-and-leaf key: 1 | 2 = 12. For back-to-back plots put A leaves on the left in reverse order (8 5 2 | 1 | 3 6).

Worked example

Library loans on four days: 12, 18, 15, 21. A line graph shows a net increase of 9, but not a steady increase. Do not extrapolate indefinitely.
