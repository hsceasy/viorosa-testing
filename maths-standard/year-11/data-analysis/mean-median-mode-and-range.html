Measures of centre

Learn the concept

Mean = sum ÷ count; median = middle ordered value (average middle pair for even count); mode = most frequent value. For frequency tables use weighted sum ÷ total frequency. Median resists extreme values; mode suits nominal categories.

Worked example

Values 2, 3, 3, 4, 18: mean = 30/5 = 6, median = 3, mode = 3. The mean is pulled upward by 18. Median better describes a typical value here.

Now you try

From the dot plot find mean, median and mode.

Show worked answer

Sum 27 ÷ 7 = 3.857…; median 4; modes 3 and 4.
