Algebraic fractions

Key idea

Use a common denominator to add fractions. For an equation with one fraction on each side, multiply both sides by both denominators (cross-multiply). Denominators cannot be zero.

Add fractions

x3 + x4

4x12 + 3x12

7x12

Cross-multiply

x+23 = x−22

2(x+2) = 3(x−2)

2x+4 = 3x−6

4 = x−6

10 = x

x = 10

Check units, brackets and each inverse operation before finalising your answer.
