Blood alcohol content (BAC)

Key idea

BAC is blood alcohol concentration, expressed here in grams per 100 mL. A classroom model is B = 10N−7.5HkM, where N is standard drinks, H is elapsed hours, M is mass in kg and k is a supplied model constant (e.g. 5.5 or 6.8). The simplified elimination model uses t = B0.015 hours to model reaching zero. These estimates cannot establish whether driving is safe or legal.
