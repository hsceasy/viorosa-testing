Rearranging formulas

Key idea

Isolate the required pronumeral using inverse operations on both sides.

Make w the subject

P = 2l+2w

P−2l = 2w

P−2l2 = w

w = P−2l2

Undo a square root

v = √(2ad), with non-negative quantities.

v² = 2ad

v² ÷ 2a = d

d = v²2a (a ≠ 0)

Undo a square

A = πr²

Aπ = r²

r = √(Aπ) because radius is non-negative.

Check units, brackets and each inverse operation before finalising your answer.
