Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Nonlinear formulas

Key idea

Isolate the power before taking a root. In measurement, choose the nonnegative root.

Worked example 1

A = πr², A = 49π → r² = 49 → r = 7.

Worked example 2

V = s³, V = 125 → s = 5.

Common mistake: Forgetting the square root.
