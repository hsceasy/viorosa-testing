Stopping distances

Key idea

Speed = distancetime; distance = speed × time; time = distancespeed. Convert km/h to m/s by dividing by 3.6. Reaction distance = speed × reaction time. Stopping distance = reaction distance + braking distance. Braking models depend on road conditions and may use d = kv² with supplied k.

Calculate stopping distance

Speed = 72 km/h; reaction time = 1.5 s; braking distance = 28 m.

v = 72 ÷ 3.6

v = 20 m/s

Reaction distance = 20 × 1.5

Reaction distance = 30 m

Stopping distance = 30 + 28

Stopping distance = 58 m

Find reaction time

Stopping distance = 55 m; braking distance = 31 m; speed = 16 m/s.

Reaction distance = 55−31

Reaction distance = 24 m

t = 2416

t = 1.5 s

Braking model

d = 0.08v², v = 20 m/s

d = 0.08(20)²

d = 0.08 × 400

d = 32 m
