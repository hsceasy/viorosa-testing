VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 11 · MATHEMATICS STANDARD

Solving linear equations

Key idea

Apply inverse operations to both sides to keep the equation balanced.

Two-step equation

5x−7 = 28

5x = 35

x = 7

Variables on both sides

7x+4 = 3x+32

4x+4 = 32

4x = 28

x = 7

Two algebraic fractions

x3 + x+22 = 6

Multiply every term by 6.

2x + 3(x+2) = 36

2x+3x+6 = 36

5x+6 = 36

5x = 30

x = 6

Check units, brackets and each inverse operation before finalising your answer.
