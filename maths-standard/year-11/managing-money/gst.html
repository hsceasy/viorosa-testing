GST and VAT

Key idea

GST is generally 10% on taxable Australian sales. VAT is a similar consumption tax used in other countries; rates vary.

Add GST

Inclusive price = exclusive price × 1.10

Remove GST

Exclusive price = inclusive price1.10

GST component

GST-inclusive price11

Worked example 1

A plumber quotes $350 before GST. Find the total.

GST = $350 × 0.10

GST = $35

Total = $350 + $35

Total = $385

Worked example 2 — unitary method

A receipt totals $264 including GST. Find the GST and original price.

110% = $264

1% = $264 ÷ 110

1% = $2.40

10% = $2.40 × 10

GST = $24

Original price = $264 − $24

Original price = $240

Worked example 3 — VAT

An overseas item costs $150 before VAT at a supplied rate of 20%. Find the inclusive price.

VAT = $150 × 0.20

VAT = $30

Total = $150 + $30

Total = $180
