Discounts and percentage change

Key idea

Write the percentage as a decimal before using a multiplier.

Percentage increase

New amount = original amount × (1 + percentage as a decimal)

Percentage decrease

New amount = original amount × (1 − percentage as a decimal)

Percentage change

changeoriginal amount × 100%

Worked example 1

A $160 jacket is discounted by 25%. Find the sale price.

25% = 0.25

Multiplier = 1 − 0.25

Multiplier = 0.75

Sale price = $160 × 0.75

Sale price = $120

Worked example 2

A $240 bicycle increases in price by 8%. Find the new price.

8% = 0.08

Multiplier = 1 + 0.08

Multiplier = 1.08

New price = $240 × 1.08

New price = $259.20

Worked example 3

A shop buys a bag for $40 and sells it for $54. Find the percentage profit.

Profit = $54 − $40

Profit = $14

Percentage profit = 1440 × 100%

Percentage profit = 35%
