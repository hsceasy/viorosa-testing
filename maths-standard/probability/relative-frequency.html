Relative frequency and experimental probability

Probability course notes

Key concept

Relative frequency estimates probability using observed results. More trials can improve the estimate, but do not guarantee an exact theoretical probability.

Relative frequency = number of occurrencesnumber of trials

Worked example 1

A spinner lands on blue 36 times in 120 spins. Find the relative frequency.

Divide occurrences by trials.

36120 = 0.30

Worked example 2

Use this estimate to predict blue outcomes in 500 spins.

Multiply the estimated probability by the number of trials.

0.30 × 500 = 150

Common mistake

A predicted frequency is an estimate, not a guaranteed result.
