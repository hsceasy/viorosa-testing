Independent and dependent events

Probability course notes

Key concept

Independent events do not change each other’s probabilities. Dependent events do. Drawing with replacement from a well-mixed bag gives independent draws; without replacement generally changes the probabilities.

For independent events: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

Worked example 1

A fair coin and fair die are used. Find P(head and six).

The outcomes are independent.

12 × 16 = 112

Worked example 2

A bag has 4 red and 2 blue counters. Find P(two red) without replacement.

After the first red, 3 red counters remain out of 5.

46 × 35 = 25

Common mistake

Mutually exclusive does not mean independent.
