Probability course notes

Key concept

A probability tree shows the possible outcomes at each stage. Multiply probabilities along a path; add the probabilities of distinct successful paths.

P(path) = product of branch probabilities

Worked example 1

A bag contains 3 pink and 2 purple counters. Draw two without replacement. Find P(both pink).

First pink: 35. After removing one pink: 24.

35 × 24 = 310

Worked example 2

Find P(one of each colour).

Add the pink–purple and purple–pink paths.

35 × 24 + 25 × 34 = 35

Common mistake

Without replacement, update the remaining counts.
