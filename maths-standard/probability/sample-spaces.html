Sample spaces and two-way tables

Probability course notes

Key concept

A sample space lists all possible outcomes. A two-way table organises counts for two categorical variables.

P(A and B) = count in both categoriestotal count

Worked example 1

Two fair coins are tossed. Find P(exactly one head).
