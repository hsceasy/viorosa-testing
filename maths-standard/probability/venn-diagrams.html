Venn diagram probability

Probability course notes

Key concept

A Venn diagram shows overlapping events. The intersection contains outcomes in both events; the union contains outcomes in either event or both.

P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)

Worked example 1

In a class of 30, 18 play sport, 12 play music and 5 do both. Find P(sport or music).

Subtract the overlap once: 18 + 12 − 5 = 25.

2530 = 56

Worked example 2

Find the probability of neither activity.

30 − 25 = 5 students do neither.

530 = 16

Common mistake

The overlap must not be counted twice.
