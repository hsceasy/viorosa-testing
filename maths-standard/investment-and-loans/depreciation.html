Depreciation

Investment and Loans course notes

Key concept

Reducing balance depreciation applies a percentage to the current value.

V = P(1−r)ⁿ

Worked example 1

A $10000 asset depreciates 10% annually for 2 years.

10000(0.9)²=$8100.

Worked example 2

Straight-line depreciation is $500 yearly on a $4000 asset. Find value after 3 years.

4000−1500=$2500.

Common mistake

Do not confuse straight-line and reducing balance.

Practise Investment and Loans

Compare depreciation methods

Straight-line: S = V₀ − Dn. Declining balance: S = V₀(1 − r)n.

A $20,000 asset loses $2,000 each year under straight-line depreciation; at 10% declining balance it loses $2,000 in year 1 but only $1,800 in year 2.
