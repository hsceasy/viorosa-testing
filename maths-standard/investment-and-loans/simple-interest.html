Simple interest

Investment and Loans course notes

Key concept

Interest is calculated on the original principal.

I = Prn

Worked example 1

Find interest on $2000 at 5% p.a. for 3 years.

2000×0.05×3=$300.

Worked example 2

Find final balance for $800 at 4% for 2 years.

800+800×0.04×2=$864.

Common mistake

Convert percentages to decimals.

Practise Investment and Loans

Comparing rates and terms

For $2,000, 4% earns $80 each year; 6% earns $120. The gradient is annual interest, and the vertical intercept is the principal.

For 9 months at 6% p.a., use n = 9 ÷ 12 = 0.75 years: I = 2000 × 0.06 × 0.75 = $90.
