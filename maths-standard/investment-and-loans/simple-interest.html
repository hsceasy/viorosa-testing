Simple interest

Investment and Loans course notes

Key concept

Interest is calculated on the original principal.

I = Prn

Worked example 1

Find interest on $2000 at 5% p.a. for 3 years.

2000×0.05×3=$300.

Worked example 2

Find final balance for $800 at 4% for 2 years.

800+800×0.04×2=$864.

Common mistake

Convert percentages to decimals.
