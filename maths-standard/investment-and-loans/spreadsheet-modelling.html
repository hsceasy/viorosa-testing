Investment and depreciation spreadsheets

Enter principal 2000 in B1 and annual rate 0.06 in B2. In A5 enter 0, A6 enter =A5+1 and fill down. In B5 enter =$B$1*(1+$B$2*A5) for simple interest; in C5 enter =$B$1*(1+$B$2)^A5 for compound interest. Fill down and insert a line chart with time on the horizontal axis.

For depreciation use =MAX(0,$B$1-120*A5) and =$B$1*(1-$B$2)^A5. Change rate or term and compare graphs.

Reducing balance spreadsheet

Put loan amount 15000 in H1, annual rate 4.5% in H2 and payment 500 in H3. Columns A–E: month, opening balance, interest, payment, closing balance.

Row 2: A2 = 1; B2 = $H$1; C2 = ROUND(B2*$H$2/12,2); D2 = MIN($H$3,B2+C2); E2 = B2+C2-D2. Row 3: A3 = A2+1; B3 = E2; copy C2:E2 down. Fill until zero. SUM the payment column for total paid; subtract principal for interest. Vary payment, or add a lump sum to the payment in one row (capped at amount owing). If payment is no greater than interest, the loan will not reduce.
