Reducing balance loans

Investment and Loans course notes

Key concept

Interest is charged on the outstanding balance before the repayment.

B next = B(1+r) − R

Worked example 1

A $1000 loan has 1% monthly interest and $200 repayment. Find new balance.

1000×1.01−200=$810.

Worked example 2

Find next balance.

810×1.01−200=$618.10.

Common mistake

Interest changes as balance changes.

Worked comparison: repayment strategies

Borrow $5,000 at a nominal annual rate of 12%, with interest charged before each payment. Round interest to cents each period and cap the final payment at the amount owing. Monthly rate = 1%; weekly rate = 12% ÷ 52. Weekly payment = $6,000 ÷ 52 ≈ $115.38.
