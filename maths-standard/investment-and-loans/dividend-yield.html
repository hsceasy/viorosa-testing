Dividend yield

Investment and Loans course notes

Key concept

Dividend yield compares annual dividend per share with share price.

Yield = annual dividendshare price × 100%

Worked example 1

A $20 share pays $1 annually.

120×100%=5%.

Worked example 2

Find annual dividend income on 300 shares paying $0.80 each.

300×0.80=$240.

Common mistake

Use annual dividend, not one quarterly payment.
