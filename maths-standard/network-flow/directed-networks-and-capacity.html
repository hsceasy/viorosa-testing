Directed networks and capacity

Network Flow course notes

Key concept

Arrows show allowed flow direction. Capacity is the maximum permitted flow on an edge.

0 ≤ flow ≤ capacity

Worked example 1

An edge has capacity 8 and flow 5. Find spare capacity.

8−5=3.

Worked example 2

Can flow 9 pass through that edge?

No, 9 exceeds 8.

Common mistake

Capacity is not necessarily actual flow.
