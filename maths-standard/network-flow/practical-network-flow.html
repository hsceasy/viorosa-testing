Practical network flow problems

Network Flow course notes

Key concepts

Compare the proven maximum flow with demand in the same units. Identify a limiting cut before recommending upgrades.

Shortfall = max(0, demand − maximum flow)

Worked example

A town needs 16 L/s. Its two independent water routes have capacities 10 then 6 and 8 then 7 L/s. Can it meet demand? Suggest a sufficient upgrade.

Worked solution
  1. Upper route = 6; lower route = 7.
  2. Maximum = 13 L/s; sink cut = 6+7=13.
  3. Shortfall = 16−13=3 L/s.
  4. Increase A→T from 6 to 9. New maximum = min(10,9)+7=16 L/s.
  5. The upgraded network meets demand exactly.

Now you try

A parcel depot needs 15 parcels/min. Independent routes have capacities 8 then 7 and 9 then 5. Find the shortfall and propose a sufficient upgrade.

Show answer
  1. Maximum = 7+5=12 parcels/min.
  2. Shortfall = 15−12=3.
  3. Increase the lower final edge from 5 to 8.
  4. New maximum = 7+8=15 parcels/min; demand is met.
