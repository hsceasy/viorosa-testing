Exponential growth graphs

Algebraic Relationships course notes

Key concept

Exponential growth multiplies a positive starting value by the same factor each step. The graph rises increasingly steeply.

y = Abˣ, A > 0, b > 1

Worked example 1

Describe y = 2ˣ.

The y-intercept is (0, 1). There is no x-intercept. The horizontal asymptote is y = 0; the graph approaches it as x decreases.

Worked example 2

A population starts at 200 and grows by 10% per year. Find the population after 2 years.

200 × 1.1² = 242.

Common mistake

A 10% increase means multiply by 1.10, not 0.10.
