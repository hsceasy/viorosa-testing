Scale drawings and proportion

Ratios and Rates course notes

Key concept

A scale relates corresponding lengths; area scales by the square of the length factor.

Actual length = drawing length × scale factor

Worked example 1

At 1:200, a line is 4 cm.

800 cm=8 m.

Worked example 2

At 1:10, drawing area is 3 cm².

Actual area=300 cm².

Common mistake

Do not use the length factor for area.
