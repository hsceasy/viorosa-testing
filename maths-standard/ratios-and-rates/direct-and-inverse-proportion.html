Direct and inverse proportion

Ratios and Rates course notes

Key concept

Direct proportion has constant ratio; inverse proportion has constant product.

y = kx or y = kx

Worked example 1

If y=12 when x=3 and direct, find y at x=5.

k=4; y=20.

Worked example 2

If y=12 when x=3 and inverse, find y at x=6.

k=36; y=6.

Common mistake

Identify the type before calculating.
