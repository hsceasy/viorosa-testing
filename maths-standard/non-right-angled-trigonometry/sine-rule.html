Sine rule

Non-right-angled Trigonometry course notes

Key concept

Pair each side with its opposite angle. Use a known opposite pair to find another side or angle.

asin A = bsin B

Worked example 1

Find a when A=30°, B=45°, b=10 cm.

a = 10 sin 30° / sin 45° = 7.07 cm.

Worked example 2

Find acute A when a=6, b=10, B=30°.

sin A = 6 sin 30° / 10 = 0.3; A ≈ 17.46°.

Common mistake

Check for a possible supplementary angle when finding angles.
