Triangle area

Non-right-angled Trigonometry course notes

Key concept

Use two sides and their included angle to calculate area.

Area = ½ab sin C

Worked example 1

Find area when a=8, b=10, C=30°.

Area=½×8×10×0.5=20 cm², if lengths are in cm.

Worked example 2

Find area when a=6 cm, b=9 cm, C=90°.

Area=27 cm².

Common mistake

Use square units.
