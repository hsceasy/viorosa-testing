True and compass bearings

Trigonometry course notes

Key concepts

True bearings are clockwise from north, written with three digits. Compass bearings start at N or S and turn toward E or W. Reverse bearings differ by 180° modulo 360°.

Formula reference

NE: θ = compass angle
SE: θ = 180° − compass angle
SW: θ = 180° + compass angle
NW: θ = 360° − compass angle

Worked example 1

Convert S35°E to a true bearing.

180° − 35° = 145°T.

Worked example 2

Convert 310°T to a compass bearing and find its reverse true bearing.

N50°W; reverse = 130°T.
