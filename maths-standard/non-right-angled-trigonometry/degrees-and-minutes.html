Degrees and minutes

Trigonometry course notes

Key concepts

One degree contains 60 minutes. Minutes are not decimal hundredths. Convert to decimal degrees before using trig, then convert the fractional degree back to minutes.

Formula reference

decimal degrees = degrees + minutes60
minutes = fractional degree × 60

Worked example 1

Convert 42° 18′ to decimal degrees.

42 + 18 ÷ 60 = 42.3°.

Worked example 2

Convert 37.625° to the nearest minute.

0.625 × 60 = 37.5′, so 37° 38′.

Worked example 3

Find the opposite side for 30° 30′ and adjacent 10 m.

10 tan 30.5° ≈ 5.89 m.
