Present value of an annuity

Annuities course notes

Key concept

Present value discounts future equal payments to today.

PV = R[1−(1+r)⁻ⁿ]/r

Worked example 1

Find present value of two $110 year-end payments at 10%.

1101.1+1101.21≈$190.91.

Worked example 2

Find present value of one $525 payment in a year at 5%.

5251.05=$500.

Common mistake

Discount rather than compound future payments.
