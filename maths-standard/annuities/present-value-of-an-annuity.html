Formula and factor table

PV = R × P
P = 1 − (1 + r)−nr

P is the present value factor. It discounts equal future payments to today.

Worked example

To withdraw $600 annually for five years at 3%, use the factor 4.5797.

PV = 600 × 4.5797 = $2,747.82 using the table.

At zero interest, P = n.
