Annuity balance recurrence

Annuities course notes

Key concept

Update the balance by applying interest then adding deposits or subtracting withdrawals.

B next = B(1+r) ± R

Worked example 1

Start with $1000, earn 5%, withdraw $200.

1050−200=$850.

Worked example 2

Repeat for another year.

850×1.05−200=$692.50.

Common mistake

Check whether payment occurs at the start or end.
