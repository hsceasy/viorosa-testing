VIOROSA MATHS · YEAR 12 MATHEMATICS STANDARD 2

Periodic interest rates

Annuities course notes

Key concept

For a nominal annual rate convertible monthly, divide by 12.

r = nominal annual rateperiods per year

Worked example 1

Find monthly rate for nominal 6% p.a.

6%12=0.5%.

Worked example 2

Find number of monthly payments in 4 years.

4×12=48.

Common mistake

Do not divide an effective annual rate by 12.
