Extreme outcomes

The Normal Distribution course notes

Key concepts

More extreme in either direction means a greater absolute z-score. For a specified direction use only that tail. Expected counts are approximate, not guaranteed.

Expected count = probability × number of observations

Worked example

A factory produces 2000 rods with mean length 100 cm and SD 2 cm. Estimate how many are more than 4 cm from the mean.
Worked solution
  1. 4 cm is two SD.
  2. Outside the central 95% is 5%.
  3. 2000×0.05=100 rods approximately.

Now you try

A school has 600 test scores with mean 60 and SD 10. Estimate how many exceed 80 and how many are more than 20 points from the mean.
Show answer
  1. Above 80: upper tail 2.5%; 600×0.025=15.
  2. Outside 40–80: 5%; 600×0.05=30.
