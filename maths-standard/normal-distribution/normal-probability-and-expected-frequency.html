Probability and shaded regions

The Normal Distribution course notes

Key concepts

Use the empirical rule and symmetry for integer z boundaries from −3 to 3. The central area between z=−1 and 1 is 68%; each outer tail is 16%. Between z=1 and 2 the area is 13.5%.

Tail beyond +k = (100% − central percentage) ÷ 2

Worked example

A battery lasts a mean 100 h with SD 10 h. Estimate the probability it lasts between 110 and 120 h.
Worked solution
  1. z boundaries are 1 and 2.
  2. Subtract central areas: (95%−68%)÷2=13.5%.
  3. Probability ≈ 0.135.

Now you try

A bus trip has mean 30 min and SD 4 min. Estimate the probability it takes less than 22 min.
Show answer
  1. z=(22−30)÷4=−2.
  2. Lower tail=(100%−95%)÷2=2.5%.
  3. Probability ≈ 0.025.
