Standard scores

The Normal Distribution course notes

Key concept

A z-score measures distance from the mean in standard deviations.

z = (x−μ)/σ

Worked example 1

Mean 70, SD 10, score 85. Find z.

(85−70)/10=1.5.

Worked example 2

Mean 40, SD 4, z=−2. Find score.

40−8=32.

Common mistake

A negative z-score is below the mean.
