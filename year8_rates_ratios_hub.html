Lesson 01

Simplifying ratios

Convert to common units, then divide both terms by their highest common factor.

Worked example

Simplify 18:24.

18:24 = (18 ÷ 6):(24 ÷ 6)
= 3:4

Now you try

Simplify 20:35.

Show worked solution

20:35 = (20 ÷ 5):(35 ÷ 5)
= 4:7

Lesson 02

Equivalent ratios

Multiply or divide every term by the same nonzero number.

Worked example

Complete 3:5 = 12:x.

12 ÷ 3 = 4
x = 5 × 4
x = 20

Now you try

Complete 4:7 = 16:x.

Show worked solution

16 ÷ 4 = 4
x = 7 × 4
x = 28

Lesson 03

Sharing in a ratio

Add the ratio parts, find one part, then multiply.

Worked example

Share $84 in the ratio 3:4.

3 + 4 = 7 parts
84 ÷ 7 = 12
3 × 12 = $36
4 × 12 = $48

Now you try

Share $60 in the ratio 2:3. Find the smaller share.

Show worked solution

2 + 3 = 5
60 ÷ 5 = 12
2 × 12 = $24

Lesson 04

Ratios with units

Convert quantities to the same unit before simplifying.

Worked example

Simplify 2 m:50 cm.

2 m = 200 cm
200:50 = (200 ÷ 50):(50 ÷ 50)
= 4:1

Now you try

Simplify 3 m:75 cm.

Show worked solution

3 m = 300 cm
300:75 = 4:1

Lesson 05

Unit rates

Divide the quantity by the number of units.

Worked example

Find the cost per kg for 3 kg costing $12.60.

Rate = 12.603
= $4.20/kg

Now you try

Find the cost per kg for 4 kg costing $18.

Show worked solution

Rate = 184
= $4.50/kg

Lesson 06

Best buys

Compare prices per identical unit.

Worked example

Which is cheaper per kg: 2 kg for $9 or 3 kg for $12?

A = 9 ÷ 2 = $4.50/kg
B = 12 ÷ 3 = $4/kg
B is cheaper.

Now you try

Which is cheaper per kg: A, 2 kg for $10; B, 4 kg for $18?

Show worked solution

A = 10 ÷ 2 = $5/kg
B = 18 ÷ 4 = $4.50/kg
B is cheaper.

Lesson 07

Direct proportion

When quantities are directly proportional, their ratio is constant.

Worked example

5 tickets cost $35. Find the cost of 8.

35 ÷ 5 = $7 per ticket
8 × 7 = $56

Now you try

3 tickets cost $24. Find the cost of 7.

Show worked solution

24 ÷ 3 = $8
7 × 8 = $56

Lesson 08

Scale drawings

A scale of 1:n means one drawing unit represents n actual units.

Worked example

A 1:200 plan shows 4 cm. Find actual length.

4 × 200 = 800 cm
800 ÷ 100 = 8 m

Now you try

A 1:500 plan shows 3 cm. Find actual length.

Show worked solution

3 × 500 = 1500 cm
1500 ÷ 100 = 15 m

Lesson 09

Speed, distance and time

Average speed is total distance divided by total elapsed time.

Worked example

Travel 150 km in 2.5 h. Find average speed.

Speed = 1502.5
= 60 km/h

Now you try

Travel 180 km in 3 h. Find average speed.

Show worked solution

Speed = 1803
= 60 km/h

Lesson 10

Converting rates

To convert km/h to m/s, multiply by 1000 and divide by 3600.

Worked example

Convert 72 km/h to m/s.

72 × 1000 = 72000 m/h
72000 ÷ 3600 = 20 m/s

Now you try

Convert 36 km/h to m/s.

Show worked solution

36 × 1000 = 36000 m/h
36000 ÷ 3600 = 10 m/s

Lesson 11

Distance–time graphs

The slope of a distance-from-start graph represents signed rate of change; a horizontal segment means stopped.

Worked example

Distance increases from 0 to 60 km in 2 h. Find speed.

Change in distance = 60 − 0 = 60 km
Change in time = 2 − 0 = 2 h
Speed = 60 ÷ 2 = 30 km/h

Now you try

Distance increases 80 km in 2 h. Find speed.

Show worked solution

80 ÷ 2 = 40 km/h

Lesson 12

Multi-stage journeys

Include stops when calculating average speed over the whole journey.

Worked example

Travel 60 km in 1 h, stop 30 min, then travel 30 km in 0.5 h.

Total distance = 60 + 30 = 90 km
Total time = 1 + 0.5 + 0.5 = 2 h
Average speed = 90 ÷ 2 = 45 km/h

Now you try

Travel 100 km in 2 h and stop 0.5 h. Find overall average speed.

Show worked solution

Total time = 2 + 0.5 = 2.5 h
Average speed = 100 ÷ 2.5 = 40 km/h
