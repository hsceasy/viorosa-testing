Annuities reference guide

Ordinary annuities use equal payments at the end of each period. R is the payment, r is the decimal interest rate per period and n is the number of periods.

Future value

FV = R × (1+r)n−1r

Present value

PV = R × 1−(1+r)−nr

Balance recurrence

Bnext = B(1+r) + R (deposits) or B(1+r) − R (withdrawals).

Interest rate

Periodic rate = annual nominal rateperiods per year
