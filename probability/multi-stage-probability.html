Multi-stage probability and at least one event

Probability course notes

Key concept

For experiments with multiple stages, identify successful paths and add their probabilities. For at least one success, the complement is often quicker.

P(at least one success) = 1 − P(no successes)

Worked example 1

A player has an independent 0.8 chance of scoring each shot. Find P(at least one score in two shots).

P(no scores) = 0.2 × 0.2 = 0.04.

1 − 0.04 = 0.96

Worked example 2

Find P(exactly one score).

There are two successful paths: score–miss and miss–score.

0.8 × 0.2 + 0.2 × 0.8 = 0.32

Common mistake

Include every distinct successful path, but do not count a path twice.
