How to calculate probability

Probability course notes

Key concept

Probability measures how likely an event is. It ranges from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). For equally likely outcomes, divide favourable outcomes by all possible outcomes.

P(A) = favourable outcomestotal outcomes

Worked example 1

A fair die is rolled. Find the probability of an even number.

Even outcomes: 2, 4, 6. There are 3 favourable outcomes out of 6.

P(even) = 36 = 12 = 50%

Worked example 2

A bag has 4 red and 6 blue counters. Find P(red).

There are 10 counters in total.

P(red) = 410 = 0.4

Common mistake

Only count equally likely outcomes when using the counting formula.
