Complementary events in probability

Probability course notes

Key concept

The complement of an event A consists of all outcomes where A does not occur. Together the event and its complement have probability 1.

P(not A) = 1 − P(A)

Worked example 1

The probability of rain is 0.35. Find the probability of no rain.

Subtract from 1.

1 − 0.35 = 0.65

Worked example 2

A fair die is rolled twice. Find the probability of at least one six.

Find the probability of no six on either roll: 56 × 56.

1 − 2536 = 1136

Common mistake

At least one means one or more, not exactly one.
