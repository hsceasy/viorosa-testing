Statistical reference guide

Describe association using direction, strength and form. Pearson’s r measures linear association. A fitted line y = mx + c predicts the response; m is the predicted change per unit of x. Interpolation is within the observed input range; extrapolation is outside it. Correlation does not establish causation.
