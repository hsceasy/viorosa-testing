MA4-RAT-C-01 · Learn, practise and mark each lesson complete to grow your blue cornflower.

Key terms
Ratio
A comparison of quantities in a specified order.
Rate
A comparison of quantities with different units.
Unit rate
A rate expressed per one unit.
Equivalent ratios
Ratios with the same relative quantities.
Unitary method
Finding one unit before scaling to the required amount.
Scale
The ratio of a drawing length to actual length.
Average speed
Total distance travelled divided by total elapsed time.
Lesson 01

Understanding ratios

A ratio compares quantities in a stated order. Use the same units before comparing quantities of the same kind.

Worked example

There are 8 pink and 12 green beads. Write pink to green.

8:12 = 2:3.

Now you try

Write green to pink.

Show worked solution

12:8 = 3:2.

Lesson 02

Equivalent and simplified ratios

Multiply or divide every term by the same positive number. Divide by the HCF for simplest form.

Worked example

Simplify 24:36:60.

Divide by 12 to get 2:3:5.

Now you try

Simplify 18:27.

Show worked solution

Divide by 9: 2:3.

Lesson 03

Ratios with different units

Convert quantities to a common unit before simplifying.

Worked example

Simplify 2 m:50 cm.

200:50 = 4:1.

Now you try

Simplify 750 g:1.5 kg.

Show worked solution

750:1500 = 1:2.

Lesson 04

Sharing in a ratio

Add the ratio terms, divide the total by that sum, then multiply by each term.

Worked example

Share $90 in the ratio 2:3.

90 ÷ 5 = 18; shares are $36 and $54.

Now you try

Share 84 in the ratio 3:4.

Show worked solution

84 ÷ 7 = 12; shares 36 and 48.

Lesson 05

Unitary method

Find the value of one unit first, then multiply by the required number.

Worked example

Five pens cost $12. Find the cost of eight.

12 ÷ 5 = 2.40; 8 × 2.40 = $19.20.

Now you try

Six tickets cost $42. Find the cost of nine.

Show worked solution

$63.

Lesson 06

Understanding rates

A rate compares quantities with different units. A unit rate gives the amount per one unit.

Worked example

A tap fills 28 L in 4 min. Find its rate.

28 ÷ 4 = 7 L/min.

Now you try

Find the rate for 45 pages in 5 min.

Show worked solution

9 pages/min.

Lesson 07

Best buys

Compare prices for the same quantity, using consistent units.

Worked example

500 g costs $4; 750 g costs $5.25. Which is cheaper per kg?

$8/kg versus $7/kg; the 750 g pack.

Now you try

2 L costs $6; 3 L costs $8.40. Which is better?

Show worked solution

3 L: $2.80/L versus $3/L.

Lesson 08

Speed, distance and time

Speed = distance ÷ time; distance = speed × time; time = distance ÷ speed. Match the units.

Worked example

Travel 36 km in 45 min. Find average speed.

45 min = 0.75 h; 36 ÷ 0.75 = 48 km/h.

Now you try

Travel 15 km at 12 km/h. Find time.

Show worked solution

1.25 h = 75 min.

Lesson 09

Converting rates

To convert km/h to m/s, divide by 3.6; reverse by multiplying by 3.6.

Worked example

Convert 54 km/h to m/s.

54 ÷ 3.6 = 15 m/s.

Now you try

Convert 8 m/s to km/h.

Show worked solution

28.8 km/h.

Lesson 10

Distance–time graphs

Time goes on the horizontal axis and distance from the start on the vertical axis. A flat segment means stationary; a steeper segment means faster motion. A falling segment means returning towards the start.

Worked example

A graph rises from (0 h, 0 km) to (2 h, 10 km), then stays flat until 3 h.

Speed is 5 km/h for two hours, then stationary for one hour.

Now you try

Distance from start falls from 12 km to 4 km in 2 h. Find speed.

Show worked solution

8 ÷ 2 = 4 km/h towards the start.

Lesson 11

Constructing distance–time graphs

Plot each time and distance pair, label axes and units, and join consecutive points with straight segments when speed is constant.

Worked example

Walk 4 km in 1 h, rest 1 h, then return in 1 h.

Plot (0,0), (1,4), (2,4), (3,0).

Now you try

Walk 6 km in 2 h, then rest 30 min. Give the coordinates.

Show worked solution

(0,0), (2,6), (2.5,6), using hours and kilometres.

Lesson 12

Scale and proportional reasoning

A scale of 1:n means one drawing unit represents n actual units. Convert units after multiplying or dividing.

Worked example

At 1:500, a drawing length is 8 cm.

8 × 500 = 4000 cm = 40 m.

Now you try

At 1:200, represent 10 m on a plan.

Show worked solution

1000 ÷ 200 = 5 cm.

Simplifying ratios · VM-RR-073
A craft kit contains 32 pink beads and 36 green beads. Write the pink-to-green ratio in simplest form.

Divide both terms by their highest common factor, 4.

Answer: 8:9

Equivalent ratios · VM-RR-074
A paint mix uses 8 cups of white paint for every 9 cups of blue. How many cups of blue are needed for 32 cups of white?

The scale factor is 32 ÷ 8 = 4. Multiply 9 by 4.

Answer: 36 cups

Sharing in a ratio · VM-RR-075
Two clubs share 68 tickets in the ratio 8:9. How many does each receive?

There are 17 parts. Each part is 68 ÷ 17 = 4 tickets. Multiply by each ratio term.

Answer: 32 and 36 tickets

Converting units · VM-RR-076
Simplify the ratio of 8 metres to 90 centimetres.

Convert 8 m to 800 cm, then simplify 800:90.

Answer: 80:9

Unit rates · VM-RR-077
A printer produces 320 pages in 4 minutes. Find its rate in pages per minute.

Divide pages by minutes: 320 ÷ 4 = 80.

Answer: 80 pages/min

A costs $8 each and B costs $9 each. The difference is $1.

Answer: Pack A, $1 per notebook

Direct proportion · VM-RR-081
4 identical tiles cover 32 square centimetres. What area do 8 tiles cover?

One tile covers 8 cm². Multiply by 8.

Answer: 64 cm²

Scale drawings · VM-RR-082
A map uses 1 cm to represent 8 km. Two towns are 4 cm apart on the map. Find their actual distance.

Multiply map distance 4 by 8 km per cm.

Answer: 32 km

Multi-step ratios · VM-RR-083
A drink contains juice and water in the ratio 8:9. There are 68 mL in the mixture. If 32 mL of water is added, what is the new juice-to-water ratio in simplest form?

Each original part is 4 mL. Juice is 32 mL; water becomes 68 mL. Divide both by 4.

Answer: 8:17

Multi-step rates · VM-RR-084

Total distance is 680 km and total time is 8 h. Divide total distance by total time and round.

Answer: 85.0 km/h
