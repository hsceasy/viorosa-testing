Lesson 02

Discovering π

The ratio of circumference to diameter is constant: π. Measure several circular objects to investigate this ratio.

Worked example

A circle has circumference about 31.4 cm and diameter 10 cm. Estimate π.

π ≈ Cd
= 31.410
= 3.14

Now you try

Estimate π when C ≈ 62.8 cm and d = 20 cm.

Show worked solution

π ≈ 62.820
= 3.14

Lesson 03

Circumference

Circumference is the distance around a circle. C = πd = 2πr.

Worked example

Find C for r = 7 cm.

C = 2πr
= 2 × π × 7
= 14π cm
≈ 43.98 cm

Now you try

Find C for d = 12 cm.

Show worked solution

C = πd
= π × 12
= 12π cm

Lesson 04

Finding radius or diameter

Rearrange C = 2πr or C = πd.

Worked example

Find r when C = 18π cm.

18π = 2πr
18π2π = 2πr2π
9 = r
r = 9 cm

Now you try

Find d when C = 15π cm.

Show worked solution

15π = πd
15ππ = πdπ
d = 15 cm

Lesson 05

Arc length

An arc with central angle θ is the fraction F[θ;360°] of the circumference.

Worked example

Find the 90° arc length for r = 8 cm.

L = 90360 × 2π × 8
= 14 × 16π
= 4π cm

Now you try

Find the 60° arc length for r = 9 cm.

Show worked solution

L = 60360 × 2π × 9
= 16 × 18π
= 3π cm

Lesson 06

Sector perimeter

Add the arc length and both radii.

Worked example

Find perimeter of a 90° sector with r = 8 cm.

L = 90360 × 2π × 8
= 4π cm
P = L + 2r
= 4π + 16 cm

Now you try

Find perimeter of a 60° sector with r = 9 cm.

Show worked solution

L = 60360 × 2π × 9
= 3π cm
P = 3π + 2 × 9
= (3π + 18) cm

Lesson 07

Semicircle and quadrant perimeter

Include straight edges as well as the curved boundary.

Worked example

Find perimeter of a semicircle with radius 5 cm.

P = 12 × 2πr + 2r
= 12 × 2π × 5 + 10
= (5π + 10) cm

Now you try

Find perimeter of a quadrant with radius 4 cm.

Show worked solution

P = 14 × 2π × 4 + 2 × 4
= (2π + 8) cm

Lesson 08

Area of a circle

A = πr². Use square units.

Worked example

Find area when r = 6 cm.

A = πr²
= π × 6²
= 36π cm²
≈ 113.10 cm²

Now you try

Find area when d = 10 cm.

Show worked solution

r = 10 ÷ 2
= 5 cm
A = π × 5²
= 25π cm²

Lesson 09

Area of a sector

Multiply the circle area by the fraction of a full turn.

Worked example

Find area of a 90° sector with radius 8 cm.

A = 90360 × π × 8²
= 14 × 64π
= 16π cm²

Now you try

Find area of a 60° sector with radius 6 cm.

Show worked solution

A = 60360 × π × 6²
= 16 × 36π
= 6π cm²

Lesson 10

Composite perimeter

Count only the outside boundary.

Worked example

A 10 cm by 6 cm rectangle has a semicircle attached along its 6 cm side. Find perimeter.

r = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 cm
P = 10 + 10 + 6 + π × 3
= (26 + 3π) cm

Now you try

A 8 cm by 4 cm rectangle has a semicircle attached along its 4 cm side. Find perimeter.

Show worked solution

r = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 cm
P = 8 + 8 + 4 + 2π
= (20 + 2π) cm

Lesson 11

Composite area

Add attached areas or subtract removed areas.

Worked example

A circle of radius 3 cm is cut from a 10 cm square. Find remaining area.

A = 10 × 10 − π × 3²
= 100 − 9π
≈ 71.73 cm²

Now you try

A circle of radius 2 cm is cut from an 8 cm square. Find remaining area.

Show worked solution

A = 8 × 8 − π × 2²
= (64 − 4π) cm²

Lesson 12

Applications and rounding

Keep π exact until the final calculation.

Worked example

A wheel of diameter 0.5 m rolls 20 revolutions. Find distance to 2 decimal places.

C = π × 0.5
Distance = 20 × 0.5π
= 10π m
≈ 31.42 m

Now you try

A wheel of diameter 0.4 m rolls 10 revolutions. Find exact distance.

Show worked solution

C = 0.4π m
Distance = 10 × 0.4π
= 4π m
