Formula and conversion guide

Ratio sharing

Share = total × individual partstotal parts

Use matching units before simplifying.

Unit rate

Rate = quantitytime or other unit

Convert units before comparing rates.

Speed, distance and time

Speed = distancetime; distance = speed × time; time = distancespeed

km/h to m/s: divide by 3.6; reverse: multiply by 3.6.

Scale drawings

Actual length = plan length × scale factor

For scale 1:n, area factor is n²; use consistent units.

Capture–recapture

Estimated population = first marked sample × second samplemarked recaptures

Assumes mixing, stable population and equal capture probability; recaptures must be positive.

Trapezoidal rule

Area ≈ h2 × (first offset + last offset + 2 × sum of interior offsets)

h is the equal spacing between offsets.

Triangle area

Area = base × perpendicular height2

Add non-overlapping areas; 1 ha = 10,000 m².

Rainfall

Volume (L) = area (m²) × rainfall (mm)

Multiply by collection efficiency as a decimal.

Fuel consumption

Fuel (L) = distance (km) × consumption (L/100 km)100

Fuel cost = litres × price per litre.

Electricity

Energy (kWh) = power (kW) × time (h)

Cost = energy × tariff; 1 kW = 1000 W.

Direct and inverse proportion

Direct: y = kx; inverse: y = kx

Find k from a known pair; inverse requires nonzero x.

Flow and production

Quantity = rate × time

Use consistent time units.
