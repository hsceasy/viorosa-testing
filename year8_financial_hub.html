Lesson 01

Percentage of a price

Convert the percentage to a fraction of 100, then multiply.

Worked example

Find 15% of $80.

15100 × 80
= 0.15 × 80
= $12

Now you try

Find 25% of $60.

Show worked solution

25100 × 60
= 0.25 × 60
= $15

Lesson 02

Discounts

Subtract the discount from the original price.

Worked example

A $120 jacket is discounted by 25%. Find the sale price.

Discount = 25100 × 120
= $30
Sale price = 120 − 30
= $90

Now you try

Find the sale price of a $80 item discounted by 15%.

Show worked solution

Discount = 15100 × 80
= $12
Sale price = 80 − 12
= $68

Lesson 03

Price increases

Add the percentage increase to the original price.

Worked example

Increase $50 by 12%.

Increase = 12100 × 50
= $6
New price = 50 + 6
= $56

Now you try

Increase $80 by 5%.

Show worked solution

Increase = 5100 × 80
= $4
New price = 80 + 4
= $84

Lesson 04

GST added

For a taxable sale, Australian GST is 10% of the GST-exclusive price.

Worked example

Add GST to $240.

GST = 10100 × 240
= $24
Total = 240 + 24
= $264

Now you try

Add GST to $150.

Show worked solution

GST = 10100 × 150
= $15
Total = 150 + 15
= $165

Lesson 05

GST included

The GST-inclusive price is 110% of the original. The GST component is one eleventh of the total.

Worked example

Find the GST in $220 including GST.

GST = 22011
= $20
Original = 220 − 20
= $200

Now you try

Find the GST component of $330 including GST.

Show worked solution

GST = 33011
= $30

Lesson 06

Profit

Profit is selling price minus cost price.

Worked example

Cost $45, selling price $60. Find profit.

Profit = 60 − 45
= $15

Now you try

Cost $72, selling price $90. Find profit.

Show worked solution

Profit = 90 − 72
= $18

Lesson 07

Loss

Loss is cost price minus selling price when cost exceeds selling price.

Worked example

Cost $90, selling price $72. Find loss.

Loss = 90 − 72
= $18

Now you try

Cost $120, selling price $95. Find loss.

Show worked solution

Loss = 120 − 95
= $25

Lesson 08

Profit as a percentage of cost

Divide profit by cost price, then multiply by 100%.

Worked example

Cost $80, selling price $100. Find profit percentage of cost.

Profit = 100 − 80
= $20
Percentage = 2080 × 100%
= 0.25 × 100%
= 25%

Now you try

Cost $60, selling price $90. Find profit percentage of cost.

Show worked solution

Profit = 90 − 60
= $30
Percentage = 3060 × 100%
= 50%

Lesson 09

Loss percentage

Use the specified reference price as the denominator.

Worked example

Cost $100, selling price $75. Find loss percentage of cost.

Loss = 100 − 75
= $25
Percentage = 25100 × 100%
= 25%

Now you try

Cost $80, selling price $60. Find loss percentage of cost.

Show worked solution

Loss = 80 − 60
= $20
Percentage = 2080 × 100%
= 25%

Lesson 10

Profit margin

Profit as a percentage of selling price uses selling price as the denominator.

Worked example

Cost $60, selling price $80. Find profit percentage of selling price.

Profit = 80 − 60
= $20
Percentage = 2080 × 100%
= 25%

Now you try

Cost $70, selling price $100. Find profit percentage of selling price.

Show worked solution

Profit = 100 − 70
= $30
Percentage = 30100 × 100%
= 30%

Lesson 11

Reverse percentages

Divide the final amount by its percentage multiplier.

Worked example

After a 20% discount, a bike costs $240. Find original price.

Remaining percentage = 100% − 20%
= 80%
Original = 2400.8
= $300

Now you try

After a 25% discount, a coat costs $90. Find original price.

Show worked solution

Remaining percentage = 100% − 25%
= 75%
Original = 900.75
= $120

Lesson 12

Successive changes

Apply each percentage to the current price, not the original price.

Worked example

A $100 item increases 20%, then decreases 20%.

Increased price = 100 × 1.20
= $120
Final price = 120 × 0.80
= $96

Now you try

A $200 item is discounted 10%, then another 10%.

Show worked solution

First price = 200 × 0.90
= $180
Final price = 180 × 0.90
= $162
