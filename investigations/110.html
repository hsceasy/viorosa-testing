Developing cylinder surface area

Back to learning hub · All investigations

Your investigation

Draw two circles of radius 3 cm and a rectangle representing the curved surface of a cylinder of height 8 cm. Explain the rectangle dimensions, derive the total area formula and evaluate it.

Check your reasoning

Rectangle width 6π cm, height 8 cm. Total area = 2πr² + 2πrh = 66π cm² ≈ 207.35 cm².
