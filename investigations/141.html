Collecting bivariate data

Back to learning hub · All investigations

Your investigation

Choose two numerical variables, such as the length and width of leaves. Collect at least ten paired measurements using consistent units. Enter pairs in the plot panel, describe association and discuss sampling limitations.

Check your reasoning

Answers vary. Each point must represent the same object; check units, sample size, association and possible outliers. Association does not establish causation.
