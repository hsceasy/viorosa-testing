Your investigation

Model two independent rolls of a fair six-sided die. Predict the probability that the sum is 7. Run 100 and 1000 trials, record relative frequencies and compare with your prediction. Explain why results vary.

Check your reasoning

6 of 36 equally likely ordered pairs sum to 7, so probability is 16. Relative frequencies vary and tend to stabilise near 16 with more trials.
