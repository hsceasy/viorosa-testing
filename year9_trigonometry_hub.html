Lesson 01

Side names

The hypotenuse is opposite the right angle. Opposite and adjacent depend on the reference angle.

Lesson 02

Sine ratio

Sine compares opposite to hypotenuse.

= 0.6

Lesson 06

Finding opposite sides

Choose sine when the hypotenuse is known.

Worked example

Find x opposite 30° with hypotenuse 10 cm.

sin 30° = x10
x = 10 sin 30°
= 5 cm

Now you try

Find x opposite 30° with hypotenuse 16 cm.

Show worked solution

x = 16 sin 30°
= 8 cm

Lesson 07

Finding adjacent sides

Choose cosine when the hypotenuse is known.

Worked example

Find x adjacent to 60° with hypotenuse 12 cm.

cos 60° = x12
x = 12 cos 60°
= 6 cm

Now you try

Find x adjacent to 60° with hypotenuse 20 cm.

Show worked solution

x = 20 cos 60°
= 10 cm

Lesson 08

Finding a denominator

Multiply by the unknown denominator, then divide by the ratio.

Worked example

Find hypotenuse x when opposite is 6 cm and θ = 30°.

sin 30° = 6x
x sin 30° = 6
x = 6sin 30°
= 12 cm

Now you try

Find hypotenuse x when opposite is 9 cm and θ = 30°.

Show worked solution

x = 9sin 30°
= 18 cm

Lesson 09

Finding angles

Use inverse sine, cosine or tangent to recover an acute angle.

Worked example

Find θ when opposite = 3 and adjacent = 4.

tan θ = 34
θ = tan⁻¹(0.75)
≈ 36.87°

Now you try

Find θ when opposite = 5 and adjacent = 5.

Show worked solution

tan θ = 55
θ = tan⁻¹(1)
= 45°

Lesson 10

Degrees and minutes

One degree contains 60 minutes. Multiply the fractional degree by 60.

Worked example

Convert 36.87° to nearest minute.

0.87 × 60 = 52.2
≈ 36°52′

Now you try

Convert 42.35° to degrees and minutes.

Show worked solution

0.35 × 60 = 21
= 42°21′

Lesson 11

Practical applications

Sketch a right triangle and label the known lengths and angle.

Worked example

A ramp rises 1 m over a horizontal run of 5 m. Find its angle.

tan θ = 15
θ = tan⁻¹(0.2)
≈ 11.31°

Now you try

A ramp rises 2 m over a horizontal run of 8 m. Find its angle to 2 decimal places.

Show worked solution

tan θ = 28
θ = tan⁻¹(0.25)
≈ 14.04°

Lesson 12

Similar triangles and mixed practice

Similar right triangles have equal corresponding ratios, regardless of size.

Worked example

Compare triangles with legs 3, 4 and 6, 8.

tan θ = 34 = 68
= 0.75 in both triangles

Now you try

Find the opposite side if adjacent = 12 and tan θ = 0.75.

Show worked solution

0.75 = x12
x = 12 × 0.75
= 9
