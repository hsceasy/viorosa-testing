Course notes

MA4-EQU-C-01 · Learn, practise and mark each lesson complete to grow your pink peony.

Key terms
linear equation
An equation with unknowns to the first power.
inverse operation
An operation that undoes another.
quadratic equation
An equation with highest unknown power 2.
operation
A calculation such as addition or division.
equation
A statement that two expressions are equal.
algebraic expression
A combination of numbers, variables and operations.
square root
A nonnegative number whose square equals the given nonnegative number.
Lesson 01

Expressions and equations

An equation states that two expressions are equal. An expression has no equals sign.

Worked example

Which is an equation: 3x + 2 or 3x + 2 = 8?

3x + 2 = 8 — An equation because it states equality.

Now you try

Write an equation for a number plus 9 equals 15.

Show worked solution

Let x represent the number.

x + 9 = 15 — The required equation.

Lesson 02

Equality and balance

Apply the same operation to both sides to preserve equality. Never multiply or divide by zero when solving.

Worked example

x + 4 = 11

x + 4=11
x + 4 − 4=11 − 4
x=7

Now you try

x + 6 = 10

Show worked solution
x + 6=10
x + 6 − 6=10 − 6
x=4
Lesson 03

Addition and subtraction

Undo addition by subtraction and subtraction by addition.

Worked example

x − 8 = −3

x − 8=−3
x − 8 + 8=−3 + 8
x=5

Now you try

x − 7 = 12

Show worked solution
x − 7=12
x − 7 + 7=12 + 7
x=19
Lesson 04

Multiplication and division

Undo multiplication by dividing by the nonzero coefficient. Undo division by multiplying.

Worked example

−3x = 21

−3x=21
−3x−3=21−3
x=−7

Now you try

x ÷ 4 = 6

Show worked solution
x4=6
x4 × 4=6 × 4
x=24
Lesson 05

Two-step equations

Undo the outer addition or subtraction before undoing multiplication.

Worked example

4x + 3 = 19

4x + 3=19
4x + 3 − 3=19 − 3
4x=16
4x4=164
x=4

Now you try

5x − 2 = 18

Show worked solution
5x − 2=18
5x − 2 + 2=18 + 2
5x=20
5x5=205
x=4
Lesson 06

Fractional and negative solutions

Solutions need not be positive or whole numbers. Keep exact fractions where possible.

Worked example

2x + 1 = 8

2x + 1=8
2x + 1 − 1=8 − 1
2x=7
2x2=72
x=72

Now you try

3x + 5 = −1

Show worked solution
3x + 5=−1
3x + 5 − 5=−1 − 5
3x=−6
3x3=−63
x=−2
Lesson 07

Unknowns on both sides

Subtract the same variable term from both sides to collect unknowns.

Worked example

5x = 2x + 12

5x=2x + 12
5x − 2x=2x + 12 − 2x
3x=12
3x3=123
x=4

Now you try

7x = 3x + 20

Show worked solution
7x=3x + 20
7x − 3x=3x + 20 − 3x
4x=20
4x4=204
x=5
Lesson 08

Checking by substitution

Replace the unknown with your proposed solution and compare both sides.

Worked example

Check x = 3 in 2x + 5 = 11.

LHS=2x + 5
=2 × 3 + 5
=6 + 5
=11
RHS=11

LHS = RHS, so x = 3 is a solution.

Now you try

Is x = −2 a solution of 4x + 1 = −7?

Show worked solution
LHS=4x + 1
=4 × (−2) + 1
=−8 + 1
=−7
RHS=−7

LHS = RHS, so x = −2 is a solution.

Lesson 09

Word problems

Define the unknown, form an equation, solve and interpret the answer.

Worked example

Three identical tickets plus a $2 fee cost $26.

Let x be the ticket price in dollars.

3x + 2=26
3x + 2 − 2=26 − 2
3x=24
3x3=243
x=8

Each ticket costs $8.

Now you try

Four notebooks plus $3 postage cost $23. Find each notebook price.

Show worked solution

Let x be the notebook price in dollars.

4x + 3=23
4x + 3 − 3=23 − 3
4x=20
4x4=204
x=5

Each notebook costs $5.

Lesson 10

Equations from formulas

Substitute known values, then solve for the remaining unknown.

Worked example

P = 2l + 2w, P = 26, w = 5.

P=2l + 2w
26=2l + 2 × 5
26=2l + 10
26 − 10=2l + 10 − 10
16=2l
162=2l2
8=l
l=8

Now you try

A = lw, A = 36, w = 4. Find l.

Show worked solution
A=lw
36=4l
364=4l4
9=l
l=9
Lesson 11

Simple quadratic equations

For x² = a with a > 0, both positive and negative square roots satisfy the equation. For a = 0 only zero works; for a < 0 there is no real solution.

Worked example

2x² = 50

2x²=50
2x²2=502
x²=25
x=±√25
x=±5

Both 5 and −5 satisfy the equation.

Now you try

3x² = 48

Show worked solution
3x²=48
3x²3=483
x²=16
x=±√16
x=±4

Both 4 and −4 satisfy the equation.

Lesson 12

Quadratics in context and exact answers

Keep square roots exact until rounding is requested. In a length problem only positive solutions are meaningful.

Worked example

A square has area 18 cm². Find its side.

Let s be the side length in centimetres.

A=s²
18=s²
s²=18
s=±√18
s=√18
s=3√2
s=4.24 cm (approx.)

Reject the negative root because a side length is positive. The exact length is 3√2 cm.

Now you try

Solve 2x² = 14, exact and to 2 decimal places.

Show worked solution

Divide both sides by 2, then take both square roots.

2x²=14
2x²2=142
x²=7
x=±√7
x=±2.65 (approx.)

The exact solutions are ±√7; the rounded solutions are ±2.65.
