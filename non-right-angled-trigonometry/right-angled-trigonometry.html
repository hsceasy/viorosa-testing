Right-angled trigonometry

Non-right-angled Trigonometry course notes

Key concept

Use Pythagoras or SOH CAH TOA for right triangles.

sin θ = oppositehypotenuse; cos θ = adjacenthypotenuse; tan θ = oppositeadjacent

Worked example 1

Find the hypotenuse with legs 6 and 8.

√(36+64)=10.

Worked example 2

Find θ if opposite=3 and adjacent=4.

θ=tan⁻¹(34)≈36.87°.

Common mistake

Use degree mode.
