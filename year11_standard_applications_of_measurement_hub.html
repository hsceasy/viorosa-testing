Key terms
Capacity
Internal volume, often measured in litres
Composite shape
Shape made from simpler shapes
Cylinder
Solid with two parallel circular bases
Hectare
10 000 m²
Mass
Amount of matter, measured in grams or kilograms
Parallel
Lines that remain the same distance apart
Perpendicular
Meeting at 90°
Pythagoras’ theorem
c² = a² + b² for a right triangle
Scientific notation
a × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ a < 10
Sector
Part of a circle between two radii and an arc
Significant figures
Digits counted from the first nonzero digit
Surface area
Total area of exposed surfaces
Trapezium
Quadrilateral with a pair of parallel sides
Trapezoidal rule
Estimate area by adding trapeziums
Volume
Space occupied by a solid
